1. WMA12/01 January 2024 · Q2

Easy3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Choose the x power

    In $\left(\dfrac38+4x\right)^{12}$, the term in $x^7$ comes from choosing $(4x)^7$ and $\left(\dfrac38\right)^5$.

  2. Write the term

    The coefficient is $\binom{12}{7}\left(\dfrac38\right)^5 4^7$.

  3. Simplify the powers

    $4^7=2^{14}$ and $8^5=2^{15}$, so $\left(\dfrac38\right)^5 4^7=\dfrac{3^5}{2}$.

  4. Evaluate

    $\binom{12}{7}=792$, so the coefficient is $792\cdot\dfrac{243}{2}=96228$.

Final answer

$96228$.

2. WMA12/01 May/June 2022 · Q1

  1. Write the binomial terms

    $(2+\dfrac38x)^{10}=2^{10}+\binom{10}{1}2^9\left(\dfrac38x\right)+\binom{10}{2}2^8\left(\dfrac38x\right)^2+\binom{10}{3}2^7\left(\dfrac38x\right)^3+\cdots$.

  2. Simplify the constant and linear terms

    $2^{10}=1024$ and $\binom{10}{1}2^9\left(\dfrac38\right)x=1920x$.

  3. Simplify the quadratic term

    $\binom{10}{2}2^8\left(\dfrac38\right)^2x^2=1620x^2$.

  4. Simplify the cubic term

    $\binom{10}{3}2^7\left(\dfrac38\right)^3x^3=810x^3$.

  5. State the expansion

    The first four terms are $1024+1920x+1620x^2+810x^3$.

Final answer

$1024+1920x+1620x^2+810x^3$.

5. WMA12/01 January 2026 · Q1

Medium6 marks

  1. Use the binomial expansion

    For $(3+kx)^7$, the first four terms are $3^7+\binom71 3^6(kx)+\binom72 3^5(kx)^2+\binom73 3^4(kx)^3$.

  2. Simplify the coefficients

    This gives $2187+5103kx+5103k^2x^2+2835k^3x^3$.

  3. Compare the required coefficients

    The coefficient of $x^2$ is $5103k^2$ and the coefficient of $x^3$ is $2835k^3$.

  4. Solve for k

    Since $5103k^2=3(2835k^3)$ and $k\ne0$, divide by $k^2$ to get $5103=8505k$, hence $k=\dfrac{5103}{8505}=\dfrac35$.

Final answer

$(a)\ 2187+5103kx+5103k^2x^2+2835k^3x^3.\quad (b)\ k=\dfrac35$.

  1. Write the first four terms

    $(1+px)^{10}=1+10px+\binom{10}{2}p^2x^2+\binom{10}{3}p^3x^3+\cdots$.

  2. Compare the x coefficient

    Since the coefficient of $x$ is $15$, $10p=15$, so $p=\dfrac32$.

  3. Find q

    $q=\binom{10}{2}p^2=45\left(\dfrac32\right)^2=\dfrac{405}{4}$.

  4. Find r

    $r=\binom{10}{3}p^3=120\left(\dfrac32\right)^3=405$.

Final answer

$p=\dfrac32$, $q=\dfrac{405}{4}$, $r=405$.

8. WMA12/01 October 2021 · Q1

Medium6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Compare the linear term

    In $(1+kx)^{16}$, the coefficient of $x$ is $16k$.

  2. Find k

    Since the coefficient of $x$ is $-4$, $16k=-4$, so $k=-\dfrac14$.

  3. Find p

    The coefficient of $x^2$ is $\binom{16}{2}k^2=120\left(\dfrac1{16}\right)=\dfrac{15}{2}$.

  4. Find the needed binomial terms

    For $k=-\dfrac14$, the $x^2$ coefficient in $(1+kx)^{16}$ is $\dfrac{15}{2}$ and the $x^3$ coefficient is $\binom{16}{3}\left(-\dfrac14\right)^3=-\dfrac{35}{4}$.

  5. Build the x squared term in g

    In $g(x)=\left(2+\dfrac{16}{x}\right)(1+kx)^{16}$, the $x^2$ term comes from $2\left(\dfrac{15}{2}x^2\right)$ and $\dfrac{16}{x}\left(-\dfrac{35}{4}x^3\right)$.

  6. Simplify

    The required term is $15x^2-140x^2=-125x^2$.

Final answer

$(a)(i)\ k=-\dfrac14.\quad (a)(ii)\ p=\dfrac{15}{2}.\quad (b)\ -125x^2$.

9. WMA12/01 October 2019 · Q3

Medium6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Write the binomial terms

    $(1+\dfrac{x}{4})^{12}=1+\binom{12}{1}\dfrac{x}{4}+\binom{12}{2}\left(\dfrac{x}{4}\right)^2+\binom{12}{3}\left(\dfrac{x}{4}\right)^3+\cdots$.

  2. Simplify the first four terms

    This gives $1+3x+\dfrac{33}{8}x^2+\dfrac{55}{16}x^3$.

  3. Rewrite the prefactor

    $\dfrac{x^2+8}{x^5}=x^{-3}+8x^{-5}$.

  4. Find the constant contributions

    The $x^{-3}$ part needs the $x^3$ coefficient, which is $\dfrac{55}{16}$. The $8x^{-5}$ part needs the $x^5$ coefficient, which is $8\binom{12}{5}\left(\dfrac14\right)^5=8\cdot\dfrac{99}{128}=\dfrac{99}{16}$.

  5. Add the constant terms

    The term independent of $x$ is $\dfrac{55}{16}+\dfrac{99}{16}=\dfrac{154}{16}=\dfrac{77}{8}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ 1+3x+\dfrac{33}{8}x^2+\dfrac{55}{16}x^3.\quad (b)\ \dfrac{77}{8}$.

  1. Write the first three binomial terms

    $(2+ax)^6=2^6+6(2^5)(ax)+\binom62(2^4)(ax)^2+\cdots$.

  2. Simplify the terms

    The first three terms are $64+192ax+240a^2x^2$.

  3. Expand the other factor

    $\left(3+\dfrac1x\right)^2=9+6x^{-1}+x^{-2}$.

  4. Collect constant contributions

    The constant term in $\left(3+\dfrac1x\right)^2(2+ax)^6$ is $9(64)+6(192a)+240a^2$.

  5. Use the given constant term

    $576+1152a+240a^2=576$, so $240a^2+1152a=0$.

  6. Solve for a

    $48a(5a+24)=0$. Since $a\ne0$, $5a+24=0$, so $a=-\dfrac{24}{5}$.

Final answer

$(a)$ $64+192ax+240a^2x^2$. $\quad (b)$ $a=-\dfrac{24}{5}$.

12. WMA12/01 May/June 2023 · Q4

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Find the constant term

    In $(3+px)^5$, the constant term is $3^5=243$, so $A=243$.

  2. Find B

    The coefficient of $x$ is $5(3^4)p=405p$, so $B=405p$.

  3. Find D

    The coefficient of $x^3$ is $\binom53(3^2)p^3=10\cdot9p^3=90p^3$, so $D=90p^3$.

  4. Use B equals 18D

    $405p=18(90p^3)=1620p^3$.

  5. Solve for p

    Since $p<0$, $p\ne0$. Divide by $405p$ to get $1=4p^2$, so $p=\pm\dfrac12$. Therefore $p=-\dfrac12$.

  6. Find C

    The coefficient of $x^2$ is $\binom52(3^3)p^2=10\cdot27\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac{135}{2}$.

Final answer

$(a)$ $A=243$. $\quad (b)(i)$ $p=-\dfrac12$. $\quad (b)(ii)$ $C=\dfrac{135}{2}$.

13. WMA12/01 January 2023 · Q3

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Start the binomial expansion

    $\left(2+\dfrac{kx}{8}\right)^7$ begins with $2^7+7(2^6)\left(\dfrac{kx}{8}\right)+\binom72(2^5)\left(\dfrac{kx}{8}\right)^2+\binom73(2^4)\left(\dfrac{kx}{8}\right)^3$.

  2. Simplify the terms

    This gives $128+56kx+\dfrac{21}{2}k^2x^2+\dfrac{35}{32}k^3x^3$.

  3. Use the arithmetic progression condition

    The coefficients of $x$, $x^2$ and $x^3$ are $56k$, $\dfrac{21}{2}k^2$ and $\dfrac{35}{32}k^3$.

  4. Set middle twice

    For three terms in an arithmetic progression, twice the middle term equals the sum of the outer terms: $21k^2=56k+\dfrac{35}{32}k^3$.

  5. Use k non-zero

    Since $k\ne0$, divide by $k$ and multiply by $32$: $672k=1792+35k^2$.

  6. Solve the quadratic

    So $35k^2-672k+1792=0$. The discriminant is $672^2-4(35)(1792)=200704=448^2$.

  7. Find the possible values

    $k=\dfrac{672\pm448}{70}$, giving $k=16$ or $k=\dfrac{16}{5}$.

Final answer

$(a)$ $128+56kx+\dfrac{21}{2}k^2x^2+\dfrac{35}{32}k^3x^3$. $\quad (b)$ $k=\dfrac{16}{5}$ or $k=16$.

14. WMA12/01 October 2022 · Q2

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use the remainder theorem

    Dividing by $4x-5$ means the remainder is $f\left(\dfrac54\right)$.

  2. Substitute x equals five quarters

    $f\left(\dfrac54\right)=\left(2-\dfrac{5k}{4}\right)^5=\dfrac{243}{32}$.

  3. Take the fifth root

    Since $\dfrac{243}{32}=\left(\dfrac32\right)^5$, we have $2-\dfrac{5k}{4}=\dfrac32$.

  4. Find k

    $\dfrac{5k}{4}=\dfrac12$, so $k=\dfrac25$.

  5. Expand the binomial

    With $k=\dfrac25$, $f(x)=\left(2-\dfrac25x\right)^5$.

  6. Find the first three terms

    $2^5+5(2^4)\left(-\dfrac25x\right)+10(2^3)\left(-\dfrac25x\right)^2=32-32x+\dfrac{64}{5}x^2$.

  7. Use the coefficient of x

    For a polynomial, the gradient at $x=0$ is the coefficient of $x$ in the expansion. Therefore $f'(0)=-32$.

Final answer

$(a)$ $k=\dfrac25$. $\quad (b)\ 32-32x+\dfrac{64}{5}x^2$. $\quad (c)\ -32$.

16. WMA12/01 January 2020 · Q2

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Identify the x to the fourth term

    In $(3+ax)^6$, the $x^4$ term is $\binom64 3^2(ax)^4$.

  2. Form the equation for a

    $\binom64 3^2a^4x^4=15\cdot9a^4x^4=135a^4x^4$. Since this term is $540x^4$, $135a^4=540$.

  3. Solve for a

    $a^4=4$, so the real possible values are $a=\sqrt2$ or $a=-\sqrt2$.

  4. Find the constant contribution from 1 over 81

    The constant term from $\dfrac1{81}(3+ax)^6$ is $\dfrac1{81}\cdot3^6=9$.

  5. Find the constant contribution from x to the minus six

    The $x^6$ term in $(3+ax)^6$ is $a^6x^6$. Since $a=\pm\sqrt2$, $a^6=8$, so $\dfrac1{x^6}\cdot a^6x^6$ contributes $8$.

  6. Add the constant terms

    The term independent of $x$ is $9+8=17$.

Final answer

$(a)\ a=\pm\sqrt2.\quad (b)\ 17$.

19. WMA12/01 October 2023 · Q10

Hard12 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Find the second term

    The 2nd term of $(3+2x)^6$ is $\binom61 3^5(2x)=6\cdot243\cdot2x=2916x$.

  2. Find the third term

    The 3rd term is $\binom62 3^4(2x)^2=15\cdot81\cdot4x^2=4860x^2$.

  3. Find the fifth term

    The 5th term is $\binom64 3^2(2x)^4=15\cdot9\cdot16x^4=2160x^4$.

  4. Use the geometric condition

    For three consecutive terms in a geometric series, the middle term squared equals the product of the two outer terms.

  5. Set up the equation

    $(4860x^2)^2=(2916x)(2160x^4)$.

  6. Solve for x

    For the non-zero geometric case, divide by $x^4$: $4860^2=2916\cdot2160\,x$, so $x=\dfrac{4860^2}{2916\cdot2160}=\dfrac{15}{4}$.

  7. Use the sum to infinity

    In the different geometric series, first term $=\sin^2\theta$, common ratio $=2\cos\theta$, and $S_\infty=\dfrac85$.

  8. Form the equation

    $\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{1-2\cos\theta}=\dfrac85$.

  9. Use sine squared

    $5\sin^2\theta=8(1-2\cos\theta)$. With $\sin^2\theta=1-\cos^2\theta$, this becomes $5(1-\cos^2\theta)=8-16\cos\theta$.

  10. Show the required equation

    Rearranging gives $5\cos^2\theta-16\cos\theta+3=0$.

  11. Solve for cosine

    $5\cos^2\theta-16\cos\theta+3=0$ gives $\cos\theta=3$ or $\cos\theta=\dfrac15$. The possible value is $\cos\theta=\dfrac15$.

  12. Find the second term

    The 2nd term is first term times common ratio: $\sin^2\theta(2\cos\theta)=\left(1-\dfrac{1}{25}\right)\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac{24}{25}\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac{48}{125}$.

Final answer

$(i)(a)$ $2916x,\ 4860x^2,\ 2160x^4$. $\quad (i)(b)$ $x=\dfrac{15}{4}$. $\quad (ii)(a)$ Proven. $\quad (ii)(b)$ $\dfrac{48}{125}$.
