1. WMA11/01 October 2019 · Q1

Easy5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use the sector area formula

    For a sector, $A=\frac12 r^2\theta$. Here $A=15$ and $\theta=1.25=\frac54$, so $15=\frac12 r^2\cdot\frac54$.

  2. Find the exact radius

    $r^2=\dfrac{30}{5/4}=24$, so $r=\sqrt{24}=2\sqrt6$ because the radius is positive.

  3. Find the perimeter

    Arc length is $s=r\theta=(2\sqrt6)\left(\frac54\right)=\frac{5\sqrt6}{2}$. The perimeter is $2r+s=4\sqrt6+\frac{5\sqrt6}{2}=\frac{13\sqrt6}{2}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ r=2\sqrt6\text{ cm}.\quad (b)\ \dfrac{13\sqrt6}{2}\text{ cm}.$

2. WMA11/01 May/June 2026 · Q7

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Translate the area and perimeter

    For a sector of radius $r$ and angle $\theta$ radians, $$\frac12r^2\theta=1.$$ Its perimeter is two radii plus the arc length, so $$2r+r\theta=8.$$

  2. (b) Eliminate the angle

    From the area equation, $$\theta=\frac{2}{r^2}.$$ Substitute this into the perimeter equation: $$2r+r\left(\frac{2}{r^2}\right)=8.$$ Therefore $$2r+\frac2r=8,$$ and, since $r>0$, $$r^2-4r+1=0.$$

  3. (b) Solve for the radius and apply the condition

    Complete the square: $$(r-2)^2=3,$$ so $$r=2\pm\sqrt3.$$ The condition $r>2$ selects $$r=2+\sqrt3.$$

  4. (b) Find the exact angle

    Using $\theta=\frac{2}{r^2}$, $$\theta=\frac{2}{(2+\sqrt3)^2}. $$ Since $\frac1{2+\sqrt3}=2-\sqrt3$, $$\theta=2(2-\sqrt3)^2=2(7-4\sqrt3)=14-8\sqrt3.$$

Final answer

$(a)\; \dfrac12r^2\theta=1,\quad 2r+r\theta=8.\qquad (b)\; r=2+\sqrt3\text{ cm},\quad \theta=14-8\sqrt3\text{ radians}.$

    $\text{Area}=\dfrac12(3)(3)\sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\dfrac92\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{4}\text{ m}^2$.

  5. Show AB

    By the cosine rule, $AB^2=3^2+3^2-2(3)(3)\cos\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right)=18+9=27$, so $AB=3\sqrt3$.

  6. Find AC

    In triangle ABC, $AB^2=AC^2+8^2-2(AC)(8)\cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)$, so $27=AC^2-8\sqrt3\,AC+64$.

  7. Choose the correct length

    Solving gives $AC=4\sqrt3\pm\sqrt{11}$. From the diagram, $AC$ is the longer value, so $AC=4\sqrt3+\sqrt{11}$.

  8. Find the total area

    Area of triangle ABC is $\dfrac12(8)(AC)\sin\left(\dfrac\pi6\right)=2AC=8\sqrt3+2\sqrt{11}$. Total area is $6\pi+\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{4}+8\sqrt3+2\sqrt{11}=43.236\ldots$, so $43\text{ m}^2$ to 2 significant figures.

Final answer

$(a)(i)\ 6\pi\text{ m}^2,\ (ii)\ 4\pi+6\text{ m}.\quad (b)\ \dfrac{9\sqrt3}{4}\text{ m}^2.\quad (c)\ AB=3\sqrt3\text{ m}.\quad (d)\ 43\text{ m}^2$.
