1. WMA11/01 October 2023 · Q2

Easy4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Square root a

    $a^{1/2}=\left(\dfrac{1}{64}x^2\right)^{1/2}=\dfrac18x$.

  2. Cube b

    $b=16x^{-1/2}$, so $b^3=4096x^{-3/2}$.

  3. Find 16 divided by b cubed

    $\dfrac{16}{b^3}=\dfrac{16}{4096x^{-3/2}}=\dfrac{1}{256}x^{3/2}$.

  4. Simplify ab over 2

    $\dfrac{ab}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{64}x^2\right)\left(16x^{-1/2}\right)=\dfrac18x^{3/2}$.

  5. Raise to the power -4 over 3

    $\left(\dfrac18x^{3/2}\right)^{-4/3}=8^{4/3}x^{-2}=16x^{-2}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ \dfrac18x.\quad (b)\ \dfrac{1}{256}x^{3/2}.\quad (c)\ 16x^{-2}$.

2. WMA11/01 May/June 2025 · Q1

Easy5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Simplify p

    $p=\dfrac1{16}x^4$.

  2. Simplify part (a)

    $p^{1/2}=\left(\dfrac1{16}x^4\right)^{1/2}=\dfrac14x^2$.

  3. Simplify pq

    $pq=\dfrac1{16}x^4\cdot\dfrac{40}{x^3}=\dfrac52x$.

  4. Simplify part (b)

    $(pq)^{-1}=\left(\dfrac52x\right)^{-1}=\dfrac25x^{-1}$.

  5. Simplify part (c)

    $pq^2=\dfrac1{16}x^4\left(\dfrac{40}{x^3}\right)^2=\dfrac{1600}{16}x^{-2}=100x^{-2}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ \dfrac14x^2.\quad (b)\ \dfrac25x^{-1}.\quad (c)\ 100x^{-2}$.

3. WMA11/01 May/June 2022 · Q3

Easy5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Simplify the square roots

    $\sqrt{180}=6\sqrt5$ and $\sqrt{80}=4\sqrt5$.

  2. Complete part (a)

    $\dfrac{\sqrt{180}-\sqrt{80}}{\sqrt5}=\dfrac{6\sqrt5-4\sqrt5}{\sqrt5}=2$.

  3. Rationalise part (b)

    $\dfrac{4\sqrt5-5}{7-3\sqrt5}\times\dfrac{7+3\sqrt5}{7+3\sqrt5}=\dfrac{(4\sqrt5-5)(7+3\sqrt5)}{49-45}$.

  4. Expand and simplify

    The numerator is $28\sqrt5+60-35-15\sqrt5=25+13\sqrt5$, so the expression is $\dfrac{25+13\sqrt5}{4}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ 2.\quad (b)\ \dfrac{25}{4}+\dfrac{13}{4}\sqrt5$.

4. WMA11/01 January 2020 · Q2

Easy5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use y = 3^x

    Since $y=3^x$, powers of $3^x$ can be replaced by powers of $y$.

  2. Simplify part (a)

    $3^{3x}=\left(3^x\right)^3=y^3$.

  3. Simplify part (b)

    $\dfrac{1}{3^{x-2}}=3^{2-x}=\dfrac{3^2}{3^x}=\dfrac9y$.

  4. Simplify part (c)

    $\dfrac{81}{9^{2-3x}}=\dfrac{3^4}{(3^2)^{2-3x}}=\dfrac{3^4}{3^{4-6x}}=3^{6x}=\left(3^x\right)^6=y^6$.

Final answer

$(a)\ y^3.\quad (b)\ \dfrac{9}{y}.\quad (c)\ y^6$.

5. WMA11/01 May/June 2024 · Q2

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use m equals 2 to the n

    $2^{n+3}=2^n\times2^3=8m$.

  2. Rewrite 16 as a power of 2

    $16^{3n}=(2^4)^{3n}=2^{12n}=(2^n)^{12}=m^{12}$.

  3. Collect the x terms

    $x\sqrt3-3=x+\sqrt3$ gives $x(\sqrt3-1)=3+\sqrt3$.

  4. Rationalise

    $x=\dfrac{3+\sqrt3}{\sqrt3-1}\times\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{\sqrt3+1}=\dfrac{(3+\sqrt3)(\sqrt3+1)}{2}$.

  5. Simplify

    $x=\dfrac{4\sqrt3+6}{2}=3+2\sqrt3$.

Final answer

$(i)(a)\ 8m.\quad (i)(b)\ m^{12}.\quad (ii)\ x=3+2\sqrt3$.

6. WMA11/01 January 2024 · Q4

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Substitute p equals 2 to the x

    If $p=2^x$, then $4^x=p^2$, $2^{x+3}=8p$, and $2^{x-1}=\dfrac{p}{2}$.

  2. Rewrite the equation

    $2p^2-8p=17\left(\dfrac{p}{2}\right)-4$.

  3. Clear the fraction

    Multiplying by 2 gives $4p^2-16p=17p-8$, so $4p^2-33p+8=0$.

  4. Solve the quadratic

    $4p^2-33p+8=(4p-1)(p-8)$, so $p=\dfrac14$ or $p=8$.

  5. Return to x

    $2^x=\dfrac14=2^{-2}$ gives $x=-2$, and $2^x=8=2^3$ gives $x=3$.

Final answer

$x=3$ or $x=-2$.

7. WMA11/01 October 2023 · Q3

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Rationalise the denominator

    $\dfrac{8-\sqrt{15}}{2\sqrt3+\sqrt5}\times\dfrac{2\sqrt3-\sqrt5}{2\sqrt3-\sqrt5}=\dfrac{(8-\sqrt{15})(2\sqrt3-\sqrt5)}{12-5}$.

  2. Simplify the numerator

    $(8-\sqrt{15})(2\sqrt3-\sqrt5)=16\sqrt3-8\sqrt5-3\sqrt5+5\sqrt3=21\sqrt3-14\sqrt5$.

  3. Divide by 7

    $\dfrac{21\sqrt3-14\sqrt5}{7}=3\sqrt3-2\sqrt5$.

  4. Rearrange the equation

    $(x+5\sqrt3)\sqrt5=40-2x\sqrt3$ gives $x(\sqrt5+2\sqrt3)=40-5\sqrt{15}$.

  5. Use the result from part (a)

    $x=\dfrac{40-5\sqrt{15}}{2\sqrt3+\sqrt5}=5\left(3\sqrt3-2\sqrt5\right)$.

Final answer

$(a)\ 3\sqrt3-2\sqrt5.\quad (b)\ x=15\sqrt3-10\sqrt5$.

8. WMA11/01 January 2023 · Q5

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Let p = 3^x

    Then $9^x=3^{2x}=p^2$, $3^{x+2}=9p$, and $3^{x-1}=\dfrac{p}{3}$.

  2. Substitute into the equation

    $3p^2+9p=1+\dfrac{p}{3}$.

  3. Clear the fraction

    Multiplying by 3 gives $9p^2+27p=3+p$, hence $9p^2+26p-3=0$.

  4. Solve for p

    $9p^2+26p-3=(9p-1)(p+3)=0$, so $p=\dfrac19$ or $p=-3$.

  5. Reject the negative value

    Since $p=3^x>0$, we reject $p=-3$. Therefore $3^x=\dfrac19=3^{-2}$.

  6. Find x

    $x=-2$.

Final answer

$x=-2$.

9. WMA11/01 October 2022 · Q4

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Factor f(x)

    $f(x)=x^2(2x+1)-15x=x(2x^2+x-15)$.

  2. Factor the quadratic

    $2x^2+x-15=(2x-5)(x+3)$.

  3. Solve f(x) = 0

    $x(2x-5)(x+3)=0$, so $x=0$, $x=\dfrac52$, or $x=-3$.

  4. Use the substitution for part (b)

    The equation is the same as $f(u)=0$ with $u=y^{2/3}$.

  5. Choose the valid root

    Since $y>0$, $u=y^{2/3}>0$. The valid root is $u=\dfrac52$, so $y^{2/3}=\dfrac52$.

  6. Write y in surd form

    $y=\left(\dfrac52\right)^{3/2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{125}{8}}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{10}}{4}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ x=-3,\ 0,\ \dfrac52.\quad (b)\ y=\dfrac{5\sqrt{10}}{4}$.

10. WMA11/01 October 2025 · Q4

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Rewrite the right-hand side

    $\dfrac19\sqrt{27}=3^{-2}\cdot3^{3/2}=3^{-1/2}$.

  2. Equate powers of 3

    $3^{2p-5}=3^{-1/2}$, so $2p-5=-\dfrac12$.

  3. Solve for p

    $2p=\dfrac92$, hence $p=\dfrac94$.

  4. Clear the denominator

    $x^2+1=\dfrac{36}{x^2-4}$ gives $(x^2+1)(x^2-4)=36$.

  5. Form a quadratic in x squared

    $x^4-3x^2-4=36$, so $x^4-3x^2-40=0$.

  6. Factor

    $(x^2-8)(x^2+5)=0$.

  7. Use real roots

    $x^2=8$, so $x=\pm2\sqrt2$. The equation $x^2=-5$ gives no real roots.

Final answer

$(i)\ p=\dfrac94.\quad (ii)\ x=\pm2\sqrt2$.

11. WMA11/01 October 2024 · Q2

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Simplify the power bracket

    $(2x^4)^3=8x^{12}$.

  2. Simplify the algebraic fraction

    $\dfrac{3y^3(2x^4)^3}{4x^2y^4}=\dfrac{3y^3(8x^{12})}{4x^2y^4}=\dfrac{6x^{10}}{y}$.

  3. Rationalise the left side

    $\dfrac{16}{\sqrt3+1}\times\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt3-1}=8\sqrt3-8$.

  4. Equate the surd parts

    $8\sqrt3-8=a\sqrt{27}+4=3a\sqrt3+4$.

  5. Solve for a

    $3a\sqrt3=8\sqrt3-12$, so $a=\dfrac{8\sqrt3-12}{3\sqrt3}=\dfrac83-\dfrac43\sqrt3$.

Final answer

$(i)\ \dfrac{6x^{10}}{y}.\quad (ii)\ a=-\dfrac43\sqrt3+\dfrac83$.

12. WMA11/01 May/June 2023 · Q6

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Expand the square

    $\left(r-\dfrac1r\right)^2=r^2-2+\dfrac1{r^2}$.

  2. Rationalise the denominator

    $\dfrac1{3+2\sqrt2}\times\dfrac{3-2\sqrt2}{3-2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{3-2\sqrt2}{9-8}=3-2\sqrt2$.

  3. Let r be the square root

    Let $r=\sqrt{3+2\sqrt2}$. Then $r^2=3+2\sqrt2$ and $\dfrac1{r^2}=3-2\sqrt2$.

  4. Use part (a)

    $\left(r-\dfrac1r\right)^2=r^2-2+\dfrac1{r^2}=(3+2\sqrt2)-2+(3-2\sqrt2)=4$.

  5. Take the positive square root

    Since $r>1$, $r-\dfrac1r>0$, so $r-\dfrac1r=2$.

Final answer

$(a)\ r^2-2+\dfrac1{r^2}.\quad (b)\ 3-2\sqrt2.\quad (c)$ The expression equals $2$.

13. WMA11/01 January 2025 · Q4

Hard9 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use y equals a to the x

    $a^{3x+1}=a(a^x)^3=ay^3$.

  2. Rewrite the denominator

    $\dfrac{5}{(3a^{1-x})^{-2}}=5(3a^{1-x})^2=45a^{2-2x}$.

  3. Write in terms of y

    $45a^{2-2x}=\dfrac{45a^2}{(a^x)^2}=\dfrac{45a^2}{y^2}$.

  4. Use p equals 9 to the t

    $p=9^t=3^{2t}$, so $3^{4t+2}=9(3^{2t})^2=9p^2$.

  5. Rewrite the equation

    $3(3^{4t+2}+1)=82\times9^t$ becomes $3(9p^2+1)=82p$.

  6. Obtain the quadratic

    $27p^2+3=82p$, so $27p^2-82p+3=0$.

  7. Solve for p

    $p=\dfrac{82\pm80}{54}$, so $p=3$ or $p=\dfrac{1}{27}$.

  8. Return to t

    $9^t=3$ gives $3^{2t}=3^1$, so $t=\dfrac12$. Also $9^t=\dfrac1{27}=3^{-3}$ gives $2t=-3$, so $t=-\dfrac32$.

Final answer

$(i)(a)\ ay^3.\quad (i)(b)\ \dfrac{45a^2}{y^2}.\quad (ii)(b)\ t=\dfrac12$ or $t=-\dfrac32$.
