1. WMA11/01 May/June 2023 · Q1

Easy4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Move all terms to one side

    $4x^2-3x+7\ge4x+9$ gives $4x^2-7x-2\ge0$.

  2. Factorise

    $4x^2-7x-2=(4x+1)(x-2)$.

  3. Use the sign of the quadratic

    The roots are $x=-\dfrac14$ and $x=2$. Since the quadratic opens upwards, it is non-negative outside the roots.

Final answer

$x\le-\dfrac14$ or $x\ge2$.

2. WMA11/01 January 2026 · Q3

Easy5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solve the rational inequality

    $\dfrac4z<8$ gives $\dfrac4z-8<0$, so $\dfrac{4-8z}{z}<0$.

  2. Use critical values

    The critical values are $z=0$ and $z=\dfrac12$.

  3. State the range

    A sign check gives $z<0$ or $z>\dfrac12$.

  4. Find where line and curve meet

    $x+5=x(x-3)$ gives $x^2-4x-5=0$, so $(x-5)(x+1)=0$.

  5. Combine with the domain

    The inequality $x+5\le y\le x(x-3)$ is possible when $x\le-1$ or $x\ge5$. With $x\ge-2$, the shaded x-ranges are $-2\le x\le-1$ and $x\ge5$.

  6. Graphical representation

    Draw the vertical boundary $x=-2$ and shade the two regions between the line and the parabola: from $x=-2$ to $x=-1$, and to the right of $x=5$.

Final answer

$(i)\ z<0\text{ or }z>\dfrac12.\quad (ii)$ shade the region above $y=x+5$, below $y=x(x-3)$, with $x\ge-2$.

3. WMA11/01 May/June 2023 · Q7

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Find the area of R1

    The bottom horizontal length is 5, from $x=0$ to the intercept of $y=10-2x$ at $x=5$.

  2. Use the top length

    The top horizontal length is $4-a$, because $y=2$ meets $y=10-2x$ at $x=4$.

  3. Solve for a

    Area $R_1=\dfrac12(5+4-a)(2)=9-a=\dfrac{27}{4}$, so $a=\dfrac94$.

  4. Find k

    The line $x=a$ passes through the intersection of $y=2$ and $y=kx$, so $2=ka$. Hence $k=\dfrac{2}{9/4}=\dfrac89$.

  5. Define R2

    Region $R_2$ is to the right of $x=a$, above $y=kx$, and below $y=10-2x$.

Final answer

$(a)\ a=\dfrac94,\ k=\dfrac89.\quad (b)\ x\ge\dfrac94,\ y\ge\dfrac89x,\ y\le10-2x$.

4. WMA11/01 January 2020 · Q8

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Equate the line and curve

    $k(2x-1)=x^2+2x+11$, so $x^2+(2-2k)x+(11+k)=0$.

  2. Use the no-intersection condition

    For the line not to cross or touch the curve, the quadratic in $x$ must have no real roots, so its discriminant is less than zero.

  3. Form the inequality in k

    $(2-2k)^2-4(1)(11+k)<0$. Dividing by $4$ gives $(1-k)^2-(11+k)<0$, so $k^2-3k-10<0$.

  4. Solve the quadratic inequality

    $(k-5)(k+2)<0$, so $k$ lies between the two roots: $-2<k<5$.

Final answer

$-2<k<5$.

5. WMA11/01 May/June 2026 · Q5

Medium7 marks · main topic: Quadratics
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Factor out the leading coefficient

    $-2x^2+12x+7=-2(x^2-6x)+7$.

  2. (a) Complete the square

    $x^2-6x=(x-3)^2-9$, so $-2x^2+12x+7=-2\big((x-3)^2-9\big)+7=-2(x-3)^2+25$.

  3. (b) Read the maximum from completed-square form

    Because the coefficient of $(x-3)^2$ is negative, the curve has its maximum when $(x-3)^2=0$. Hence $x=3$, and the maximum value is $y=25$, so $P=(3,25)$.

  4. (c) Find the equation of the boundary line

    The line through $O=(0,0)$ and $P=(3,25)$ has gradient $\dfrac{25}{3}$, so $l$ has equation $y=\dfrac{25}{3}x$.

  5. (c) Describe the shaded region

    Points in $R$ are below the curve, above the line, and on or to the right of the $y$-axis. Therefore $y\leq-2x^2+12x+7$, $y\geq\dfrac{25}{3}x$, and $x\geq0$.

Final answer

$(a)\ -2(x-3)^2+25.\qquad (b)\ P=(3,25).\qquad (c)\ y\leq-2x^2+12x+7,\ y\geq\dfrac{25}{3}x,\ x\geq0.$

6. WMA11/01 May/June 2024 · Q6

Medium7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Write the line as y equals

    $y-5x=75$, so $y=5x+75$.

  2. Equate line and curve

    $5x+75=2x^2+x-21$, so $2x^2-4x-96=0$.

  3. Solve the quadratic

    $x^2-2x-48=0=(x-8)(x+6)$, so $x=-6$ or $x=8$.

  4. Find the y-coordinates

    Using $y=5x+75$, $x=-6$ gives $y=45$ and $x=8$ gives $y=115$.

  5. State P and Q

    From the diagram, $P=(-6,45)$ and $Q=(8,115)$.

  6. Use the x-axis and left branch

    The shaded region is above the x-axis and on the left part of the diagram, so $y\ge0$ and $x\le0$.

  7. Apply both boundaries

    The region is below both the line and the curve, so $y\le5x+75$ and $y\le2x^2+x-21$.

Final answer

$(a)\ P=(-6,45),\ Q=(8,115).\quad (b)\ y\ge0,\ y\le5x+75,\ y\le2x^2+x-21,\ x\le0$.
