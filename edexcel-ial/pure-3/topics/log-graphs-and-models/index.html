  2. Part (b)

    \[\log_{10}y=\log_{10}(x^{-4/7})+\log_{10}\frac1{100} =\log_{10}\left(\frac1{100}x^{-4/7}\right).\]

  1. Part (a)

    The horizontal coordinate is $\log_{10}x$ and the vertical coordinate is $\log_{10}y$. The gradient of the line is

    \[m=\frac{0-4}{6-0}=-\frac{2}{3}.\]

    The vertical intercept is $4$, so the required equation is

    \[\boxed{\log_{10}y=4-\frac{2}{3}\log_{10}x}.\]
  2. Part (b)

    Taking powers of $10$ on both sides gives

    \[\begin{aligned} y&=10^{4-\frac{2}{3}\log_{10}x}\\ &=10^4\left(10^{\log_{10}x}\right)^{-2/3}\\ &=10000x^{-2/3}. \end{aligned}\]

    Here $x\gt 0$, as required for $\log_{10}x$ to exist. Therefore

    \[\boxed{y=10000x^{-2/3}},\qquad \boxed{p=10000,\quad q=-\frac{2}{3}}.\]
Final answer

(a) $\log_{10}y=4-\frac{2}{3}\log_{10}x$ (b) $y=10000x^{-2/3}$, $p=10000,\quad q=-\frac{2}{3}$

  2. Part (b)

    Exponentiating base 10 gives

    \[N=10^{0.35t+2}=10^2(10^{0.35})^t=100(10^{0.35})^t.\]

    At $t=5$ this is $0.35\ln(10)\,10^{3.75}=4531.9356\ldots$ bacteria per hour.

    \[\boxed{\left.\frac{dN}{dt}\right|_{t=5}=4.53\times10^3\ \text{bacteria per hour}}\]
Final answer

13. WMA13/01 January 2020 · Q1

Easy6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a)

    At the start of the study, $t=0$, so

    \[N(0)=\frac{900e^0}{2e^0+1}=\frac{900}{3}=\boxed{300}.\]

    There are initially $300$ toads.

  2. Part (b)

    Set $N=420$ and clear the denominator:

    \[\begin{aligned} 420&=\frac{900e^{0.12t}}{2e^{0.12t}+1},\\ 420(2e^{0.12t}+1)&=900e^{0.12t},\\ 420&=60e^{0.12t},\\ e^{0.12t}&=7. \end{aligned}\]

    Taking natural logarithms gives

    \[0.12t=\ln 7 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\frac{\ln 7}{0.12}=16.2159179\ldots.\]

    Thus $\boxed{t=16.22\text{ years}}$ to 2 decimal places.

  3. Part (c)

    Divide the numerator and denominator by $e^{0.12t}$:

    \[N=\frac{900}{2+e^{-0.12t}}.\]

    Since $e^{-0.12t}\gt 0$ for every finite $t\geq0$, the denominator is greater than $2$. Hence

    \[N\lt \frac{900}{2}=450\lt 500.\]

    The population approaches $450$ as $t$ increases without bound, so it can never reach $500$ according to this model.

Final answer

(a) $300$ (b) $t=16.22\text{ years}$ (c) $N \lt 450 \lt 500, so the population can never reach 500$
