1. WME01/01 May/June 2024 · Q1

Easy5 marks · main topic: Momentum, Impulse & Collisions
Show worked solution
  1. Use conservation of momentum

    When the string becomes taut, A and B move with the same speed $S$. Before this, only A is moving. Hence $mU=(m+3m)S=4mS$.

  2. Find the common speed

    From $mU=4mS$, cancel $m$ to get $S=\dfrac{U}{4}$.

  3. Use impulse on A

    The impulse on A equals the magnitude of its change in momentum: $I=m\left|\dfrac{U}{4}-U\right|$.

  4. Simplify the impulse

    $I=m\left(\dfrac{3U}{4}\right)=\dfrac{3mU}{4}$.

Final answer

$(a)\ S=\dfrac{U}{4}.\quad (b)\ I=\dfrac{3mU}{4}$.

  1. Define the box mass

    Let the combined mass of boxes P and Q be $M$. The lift itself has mass $m$, so the total moving mass is $m+M$.

  2. Apply Newtons second law to the whole system

    Taking upward as positive, $\dfrac{42}{5}mg-(m+M)g=(m+M)\dfrac{2g}{5}$.

  3. Find M

    Move the weight term to the right: $\dfrac{42}{5}mg=(m+M)\left(g+\dfrac{2g}{5}\right)=\dfrac{7g}{5}(m+M)$. Hence $42m=7(m+M)$ and $M=5m$.

    $\dfrac{14}{5}mg-pg=p\dfrac{2g}{5}$.

  6. Find p

    Divide by $g$: $\dfrac{14}{5}m=p+\dfrac{2p}{5}=\dfrac{7p}{5}$. Therefore $p=2m$.
