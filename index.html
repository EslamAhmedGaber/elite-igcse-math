Quick decision guide

Linear or Modular: same goal, different rhythm.

Both routes can lead to the same strong mathematics outcome. The important choice is how the student handles pressure, memory, and retakes.

Modular Mathematics A

Step by step pressure.

Unit 1 and Unit 2 are assessed separately. A student can bank unit results, resit a unit when needed, then cash in for the full qualification.
