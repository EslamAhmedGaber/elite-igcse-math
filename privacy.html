Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who runs this site

Elite IGCSE (eliteigcse.com) is run by Dr Eslam Ahmed, Assistant Lecturer, Faculty of Engineering, Cairo University. The site provides free Edexcel IGCSE and International A Level Mathematics revision resources. If you have any question about this policy, contact us on WhatsApp: +20 112 000 9622.

The short version

Everything on this site is free. You do not need an account to use any resource. Sign-in is optional and exists only so your study progress can follow you between devices. We do not take payments, we do not handle card details, and we never sell personal data.

Using the site as a guest

If you do not sign in, your study progress — the questions you have solved, your Mistake Box, saved tests and mock results — is stored only in your own browser, using local storage. It stays on your device, is not sent to us, and you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for eliteigcse.com.

If you sign in with Google

Sign-in is provided by Google through Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we store:

  • your name and email address from your Google account, and
  • your study progress (solved questions, your Mistake Box, saved tests and mock results),

so that your progress syncs to your account and is available on any device you sign in on. This data is stored in Firebase / Firestore (Google's cloud services). As the site operator, Dr Eslam can see who has signed in and how much they study — this is used to follow students' progress and help them, as stated on the homepage.

Cookies and local storage

The site uses local storage (and similar browser storage) to remember your progress, your course and display preferences, and whether you are signed in. Firebase Authentication may also use browser storage to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

WhatsApp

Some buttons on the site open WhatsApp (wa.me links) so you can message Dr Eslam, for example to ask about a question. Those messages are sent through WhatsApp and are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We only receive the messages you choose to send.

Payments

The site is free. We do not collect payments and we never ask for or handle bank card details.

Sharing your data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. Your sign-in and synced progress are processed by Google (Firebase Authentication and Firestore) only so the sign-in and sync features work.

Students and younger users

This site is made for students revising for their exams. All resources can be used without an account. Sign-in is optional; students who are not comfortable creating an account can simply use the site as a guest, with progress kept only on their own device.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. To ask for your account data (your name, email and synced progress) to be deleted, message us on WhatsApp at +20 112 000 9622 from the account holder, and we will delete it. Guest progress can be removed instantly by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
