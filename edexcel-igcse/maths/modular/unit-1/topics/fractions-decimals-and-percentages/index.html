  2. Order them

    \(0.6,\ 0.61,\ 0.62,\ 0.625,\ 0.666\ldots\)

Final answer

\(\frac35,\ 61\%,\ 0.62,\ \frac58,\ \frac23\)

6. Elite Practice · Q17

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Proportion that walk or cycle
    \[ \frac38+0.35=0.375+0.35=0.725 \]
  2. Proportion that travel by bus
    \[ 1-0.725=0.275 \]
  3. Number of students
    \[ 0.275\times n=11 \;\Rightarrow\; n=\frac{11}{0.275}=40 \]
Final answer

\(40\)

7. Elite Practice · Q21

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Change both amounts to percentages

    \(\frac25=40\%\), so the \(120\) litres fill \(65\%-40\%=25\%\) of the tank.

  2. Find 100%
    \[ 25\%\to120 \;\Rightarrow\; 100\%\to120\times4=480\text{ litres} \]
Final answer

\(480\) litres
