2. Elite Practice · Q33

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Any non-zero number to the power 0

    \(5^0=1\)

  2. (b) Root first, then power
    \[ 8^{2/3}=\left(\sqrt[3]{8}\right)^2=2^2=4 \]
  3. (c) Negative power means reciprocal
    \[ 16^{-1/2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{16}}=\frac14 \]
Final answer

(a) \(1\) (b) \(4\) (c) \(\frac14\)

3. Elite Practice · Q35

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Write everything as a power of 2

    \(8=2^3\), so \(8^4=2^{12}\). \(4=2^2\), so \(4^3=2^{6}\).

  2. Divide
    \[ \frac{2^{12}}{2^{6}}=2^{6} \;\Rightarrow\; n=6 \]
Final answer

\(n=6\)

4. May 2026 4WM1H · Q3

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Multiply: add powers
    \[20^{70}\times20^{8}=20^{78}\Rightarrow n=78\]
  2. (b) Divide: subtract powers
    \[36^{80}\div36^{5}=36^{75}\Rightarrow m=75\]
  3. (c) Power of a power: multiply
    \[(52^4)^{16}=52^{64}\Rightarrow p=64\]
  4. (d) Lower bound

    Nearest metre means \(\pm0.5\) m: lower bound \(=99.5\) m.

  5. (e) Upper bound

    One decimal place means \(\pm0.05\) s: upper bound \(=16.35\) s.

Final answer

(a) \(78\) (b) \(75\) (c) \(64\) (d) \(99.5\) m (e) \(16.35\) s
