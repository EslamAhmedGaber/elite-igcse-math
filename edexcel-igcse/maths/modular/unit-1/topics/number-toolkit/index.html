1. Elite Practice · Q37

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Each number is ten times smaller

    \(2.3\times4.7=1081\div100=10.81\)

  2. (b) Reverse the multiplication

    \(1081\div47=23\), and \(4.7\) is ten times smaller than \(47\), so \(1081\div4.7=230\).

Final answer

(a) \(10.81\) (b) \(230\)

  1. Austria spending per person

    Austria spending per person:

    \[ \frac{4.2\times 10^{10}}{8.7\times 10^6} =4827.586\ldots \]
  2. Luxembourg spending per person

    Luxembourg spending per person:

    \[ \frac{3.7\times 10^9}{6.3\times 10^5} =5873.015\ldots \]
  3. Difference

    Difference:

    \[ 5873.015\ldots-4827.586\ldots=1045.429\ldots \]
  4. Convert standard form

3. Elite Practice · Q36

Medium3 marks

  1. (a) Exactly two factors

    \(2\) and \(17\) are prime (\(15=3\times5\), \(21=3\times7\)).

  2. (b) Square numbers in the list

    \(9=3^2\) and \(25=5^2\); the odd one greater than \(10\) is \(25\).

  3. (c) Cube numbers

    \(27=3^3\)

Final answer

(a) \(2, 17\) (b) \(25\) (c) \(27\)
