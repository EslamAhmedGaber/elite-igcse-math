1. Nov 2025 4WM2H · Q25

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. The transformation y f (4

    The transformation *y* = *f*(4*x*) represents a horizontal stretch by a scale factor of $\\frac{1}{4}$. The x-coordinate is multiplied by $\\frac{1}{4}$ while the y-coordinate stays the same. $$\\text{New minimum} = \\left(8 \\times \\frac{1}{4}, 1\\right) = (2, 1)$$

  2. Answer

    (2,1)

    **Step 1: Find the vertical shift *b*.** The standard *y* = cos (*x*) graph fluctuates between  − 1 and 1, centered at *y* = 0. The given graph fluctuates between a minimum of *y* = 1 and a maximum of *y* = 3. The center line is $\\frac{3 + 1}{2} = 2$. Therefore, *b* = 2.

    **Step 2: Find the frequency multiplier *a*.** The period of the given graph completes one full cycle between *x* = 0 and *x* = 90<sup>∘</sup> (it starts at 3, dips to 1, and returns to 3 at 90<sup>∘</sup>). The standard cosine period is 360<sup>∘</sup>. $$\\text{Period} = \\frac{360^\\circ}{a}$$ $$90^\\circ = \\frac{360^\\circ}{a} \\implies a = 4$$

Final answer

*a* = 4,  *b* = 2

3. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q23

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    The graph of *y* = *a*sin (*x*+*b*)<sup>∘</sup> + *c* for 0 ≤ *x* ≤ 360 is shown. Find suitable values for *a*, *b*, and *c*.

  2. Read the maximum and minimum

    Read the maximum and minimum values from the graph to find the **amplitude** *a* and **vertical shift** *c*: $$a = \\frac{\\text{max}-\\text{min}}{2} = \\frac{4-(-2)}{2} = 3 \\qquad c = \\frac{\\text{max}+\\text{min}}{2} = \\frac{4+(-2)}{2} = 1$$

  3. Find the phase shift b

    Find the **phase shift** *b*. The standard sin  has its peak at *x* = 90°. From the graph, the maximum occurs at *x* ≈ 45°: *x* + *b* = 90° ⟹ 45° + *b* = 90° ⟹ *b* = 45°

  4. Verify at x 0 consistent

    Verify at *x* = 0: $y = 4\\sin(0+45)° = 4\\times\\frac{\\sqrt{2}}{2} \\approx 2.83$ — consistent with graph

  5. Note

    The period of the graph is still 360° (unchanged), so no horizontal stretch is applied.

Final answer

*a* = 3,*b* = 45,*c* = 1

4. Jun 2026 P2H | L-to-M Unit 2 · Q21

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1

    At \(x=1\), the point is approximately \((1,4)\). Draw a tangent there, extending at least \(3\text{ cm}\). A suitable approximate tangent is \(y=-5x+9\), through \((0.5,6.5)\) and \((2,-1)\).

    The gradient at a point is estimated by the gradient of its tangent; use two well-separated points on the line.

  2. Part (a) | Step 2
    \[\text{Gradient}\approx\frac{-1-6.5}{2-0.5}=\frac{-7.5}{1.5}=-5.\]

    Calculate change in y divided by change in x using the axis values.

  3. Part (b) | Step 1
    \[x=3+\tfrac12 f(x)\quad\Rightarrow\quad f(x)=2x-6.\]

    Rearrange so the given curve y=f(x) can be intersected by a straight line.

  4. Part (b) | Step 2

    Draw \(y=2x-6\) through \((0,-6),(3,0),(4.5,3)\) on the same grid. Read the x-coordinates of its two intersections with the curve: approximately \(2.0\) and \(4.4\).

    Each intersection has equal y-values on the line and curve and therefore solves the original equation.

Final answer

(a) Gradient \(\approx-5\).

(b) \(x\approx2.0\) or \(x\approx4.4\).
