7. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q8

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    Sale price (after 15% reduction)  = 612 Swiss francs. Find the normal price.

  2. A 15% reduction means the

    A 15% reduction means the sale price is 85% of the normal price: 612 = 0.85 × Normal price

  3. Solve for the normal price

    Solve for the normal price: $$\\text{Normal price} = \\frac{612}{0.85} = 720$$

  4. Note

    Never calculate 15% of 612 and add it on — that gives 703.80 (wrong!). Always divide by the decimal multiplier.

Final answer

Normal price  = 720 Swiss francs

15. Nov 2025 4WM2H · Q8

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Calculate normal price

    **Step 1: Calculate Normal Price for Shop A.** A 25% discount means the sale price is 75% of the normal price. 0.75 × Normal<sub>*A*</sub> = 240 $$\\text{Normal}\_A = \\frac{240}{0.75} = 320 \\text{ euros}$$

  2. Calculate normal price

    **Step 2: Calculate Normal Price for Shop B.** A discount of $\\frac{2}{9}$ means the sale price is $1 - \\frac{2}{9} = \\frac{7}{9}$ of the normal price. $$\\frac{7}{9} \\times \\text{Normal}\_B = 245$$ $$\\text{Normal}\_B = 245 \\times \\frac{9}{7} = 35 \\times 9 = 315 \\text{ euros}$$

  3. Calculate value

    Difference = 320 − 315 = 5 euros

Final answer

5 euros

21. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q5

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    Calculate Joshua’s percentage profit on the flooring job. Give answer to 1 d.p.

  2. Number of boxes of tiles

    Number of boxes of tiles needed: $$\\frac{45\\,\\text{m}^2}{1.5\\,\\text{m}^2/\\text{box}} = 30 \\text{ boxes}$$

  3. Cost of tiles 30 64

    Cost of tiles: 30 × £64 = £1920

  4. Cost of adhesive 5 12

    Cost of adhesive: 5 × £12 = £60

  5. Total cost (cost price) 1920

    Total cost (cost price): £1920 + £60 = £1980

  6. Profit and percentage profit Profit

    Profit and percentage profit: Profit = £3000 − £1980 = £1020 $$\\%\\,\\text{Profit} = \\frac{1020}{1980} \\times 100 = 51.515\\ldots\\%$$

  7. Note

    Always divide profit by the **cost price** (£1980), not the selling price (£3000). Dividing by £3000 gives 34.0% — a common but wrong answer.

Final answer

Percentage profit  = 51.5%
