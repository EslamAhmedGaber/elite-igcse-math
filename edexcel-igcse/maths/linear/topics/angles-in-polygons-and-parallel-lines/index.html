  1. Solution | Step 1

    Let \(\theta=\angle CBE\). Then \[\frac{\theta}{360}\,2\pi(4.5)=\frac3{10}\pi\quad\Rightarrow\quad\theta=12^\circ.\]

    The arc is theta/360 of a full circumference.

  2. Solution | Step 2

    \(\angle ABC=180^\circ-12^\circ=168^\circ\), so the regular polygon has exterior angle \(12^\circ\).

    A, B and E are collinear, so the sector angle is supplementary to the interior angle.

  3. Solution | Step 3
