3. Jun 2026 P2H · Q5

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1

    Triangle \(A\) has vertices \((3,4),(4,4),(3,6)\); the corresponding vertices of \(B\) are \((5,5),(7,5),(5,9)\). The lengths have doubled.

    Read corresponding vertices and compare side lengths.

  2. Part (a) | Step 2

    For enlargement factor \(2\), the centre \(O\) satisfies \(B=O+2(A-O)\), so \(O=2A-B\). Using \((3,4)\mapsto(5,5)\) gives \(O=(6,8)-(5,5)=(1,3)\).

    Find the fixed centre from a corresponding pair; the other pairs give the same centre.

  3. Part (b) | Step 1

    Add \((4,-3)\) to every vertex: \((3,4)\mapsto(7,1)\), \((4,4)\mapsto(8,1)\), and \((3,6)\mapsto(7,3)\). Plot these three points, join them with straight sides and label the triangle \(C\).

    A translation moves every point by the same vector.

Final answer

(a) Enlargement with scale factor \(2\) and centre \((1,3)\).

(b) \(C:(7,1),(8,1),(7,3)\).

5. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q4

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    \(a\) Rotate triangle A 90° anticlockwise about (1,2); label triangle B. (b) Describe fully the transformation mapping C onto D.

  2. The rule for 90 anticlockwise

    The rule for 90° anticlockwise rotation about (*a*,*b*): (*x*, *y*) → (*a* − (*y*−*b*),  *b* + (*x*−*a*)) About (1,2): (*x*,*y*) → (3−*y*,  *x*+1)

  3. Triangle A has vertices A

    Triangle A has vertices *A*<sub>1</sub>(2,7), *A*<sub>2</sub>(4,5), *A*<sub>3</sub>(2,5). Apply the rule: (2,7) → (−4,3),   (4,5) → (−2,5),   (2,5) → (−2,3)

  4. Answer

    Triangle B has vertices (−4,3), (−2,5), (−2,3)

  5. Identify corresponding vertices of C

    Identify corresponding vertices of C and D from the diagram and compute the vector: $$(4,\\,4) \\longrightarrow (-5,\\,7): \\quad \\vec{v} = \\begin{pmatrix}-9\\\\3\\end{pmatrix}$$ Check: (6,1) → (−3,4) gives the same vector $\\begin{pmatrix}-9\\\\3\\end{pmatrix}$

Final answer

Translation by vector $\\begin{pmatrix}-9\\\\3\\end{pmatrix}$
