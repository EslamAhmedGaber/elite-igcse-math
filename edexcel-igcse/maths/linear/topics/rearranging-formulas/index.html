1. Jun 2026 P2HR · Q20

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[y(4-2x)=3x+5\quad\Rightarrow\quad4y-2xy=3x+5.\]

    Multiply by the denominator, which is nonzero in the original expression.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[4y-5=3x+2xy=x(3+2y).\]

    Collect both x-terms together and factor out x.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[x=\frac{4y-5}{2y+3}.\]

    Divide by 2y+3. The inverse formula requires y not equal to -3/2; the original requires x not equal to 2.

Final answer

\(x=\frac{4y-5}{2y+3}\).

3. Jun 2026 P2H · Q18

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[a=\frac{3w-x^2}{x^2+6}\quad\Rightarrow\quad a(x^2+6)=3w-x^2.\]

    Multiply by x^2+6, which is positive for real x.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[ax^2+6a=3w-x^2\quad\Rightarrow\quad ax^2+x^2=3w-6a.\]

    Collect both terms involving x^2 on one side.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[(a+1)x^2=3w-6a\quad\Rightarrow\quad x^2=\frac{3w-6a}{a+1}.\]

    Factor out x^2 and divide by a+1, assuming a is not -1.

  4. Solution | Step 4

    \[x=\pm\sqrt{\frac{3w-6a}{a+1}}\quad(a\ne-1).\] For real solutions the radicand must be nonnegative. If \(a=-1\), the original equation instead requires \(w=-2\); then every real \(x\) satisfies it.

    Taking square roots gives both signs; handle separately the value excluded by division.

Final answer

\(x=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{3w-6a}{a+1}}\), for \(a\ne-1\) and nonnegative radicand.

4. Nov 2025 4WM2H · Q18

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Multiply both sides denominator

    $$p^2 = \\frac{7w + y}{cw + k}$$

    **Step 2: Multiply both sides by the denominator (*c**w*+*k*).** *p*<sup>2</sup>(*c**w*+*k*) = 7*w* + *y*

  2. Expand brackets

    *p*<sup>2</sup>*c**w* + *p*<sup>2</sup>*k* = 7*w* + *y*

    **Step 4: Collect all terms involving *w* on one side.** *p*<sup>2</sup>*c**w* − 7*w* = *y* − *p*<sup>2</sup>*k*

    **Step 5: Factor out *w*.** *w*(*p*<sup>2</sup>*c*−7) = *y* − *p*<sup>2</sup>*k*

    **Step 6: Divide to isolate *w*.** $$w = \\frac{y - p^2k}{p^2c - 7}$$

Final answer

$w = \\frac{y - p^2k}{p^2c - 7}$

  1. Required

    Make *k* the subject of $\\displaystyle p = \\dfrac{8k^2+5}{7-3k^2}$.

  2. Multiply both sides by (7

    Multiply both sides by (7−3*k*<sup>2</sup>): *p*(7−3*k*<sup>2</sup>) = 8*k*<sup>2</sup> + 5

  3. Expand 7 p 3 p

    Expand: 7*p* − 3*p**k*<sup>2</sup> = 8*k*<sup>2</sup> + 5

  4. Collect all k 2 terms

    Collect all *k*<sup>2</sup> terms on one side: 7*p* − 5 = 8*k*<sup>2</sup> + 3*p**k*<sup>2</sup>

  5. Factorise and solve for k

    Factorise and solve for *k*: $$7p - 5 = k^2(3p+8) \\implies k^2 = \\frac{7p-5}{3p+8}$$ $$k = \\pm\\sqrt{\\dfrac{7p-5}{3p+8}}$$

  6. Note

    Include both ± roots unless the context specifies the sign of *k*.

Final answer

$k = \\pm\\sqrt{\\dfrac{7p-5}{3p+8}}$
