  1. Solution | Step 1

    The only possible ordered number pairs are \((1,4)\) and \((2,5)\).

    The second number must be exactly 3 more than the first, not merely differ by 3.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[P(1\text{ then }4)=\frac28\times\frac17,\qquad P(2\text{ then }5)=\frac38\times\frac27.\]

  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[P(\text{one pink, one yellow})=\frac{12}{x}\frac{x-12}{x-1}+\frac{x-12}{x}\frac{12}{x-1}=\frac{24(x-12)}{x(x-1)}.\]

    Include pink-then-yellow and yellow-then-pink, without replacement.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[\frac{24(x-12)}{x(x-1)}=\frac{18}{47}\implies1128(x-12)=18x(x-1).\]

    Equate to the given probability and cross-multiply.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[18x^2-1146x+13536=0\implies3x^2-191x+2256=0.\]

    Rearrange and divide by 6.

  4. Solution | Step 4
    \[(x-48)(3x-47)=0\implies x=48\text{ or }x=\frac{47}{3}.\]

    Factorise the quadratic.

  5. Solution | Step 5

    The number of marbles must be an integer, so \(x=48\).

    Reject the non-integer root.

Final answer

\(x=48\)
