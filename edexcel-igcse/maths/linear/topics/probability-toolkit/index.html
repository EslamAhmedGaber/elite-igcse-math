  1. Solution | Step 1

    Let \(g=P(G)\). With replacement, the draws are independent. \[P(GG)=1-P(\text{at least one red})=1-\frac{39}{64}=\frac{25}{64}.\]

    The complementary event to at least one red is two greens.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[g^2=\frac{25}{64}\quad\Rightarrow\quad g=\frac58,\qquad P(R)=1-\frac58=\frac38.\]

    Multiply identical independent green probabilities and take the nonnegative root.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[P(\text{exactly one red})=P(RG)+P(GR)=\frac38\frac58+\frac58\frac38=\frac{30}{64}=\frac{15}{32}.\]

    The two possible orders are mutually exclusive and must both be included.

Final answer

\(\dfrac{15}{32}\)

Final answer
