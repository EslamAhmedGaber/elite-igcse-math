6. Jun 2026 P1HR · Q22

Medium4 marks

  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[\angle ABC=180^\circ-62^\circ-47^\circ=71^\circ.\]

    The three triangle angles sum to 180 degrees.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[\frac{AB}{\sin47^\circ}=\frac{13}{\sin62^\circ}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad AB=\frac{13\sin47^\circ}{\sin62^\circ}=10.7680208859\ldots\text{ cm}.\]

    Match each side to its opposite angle in the sine rule.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[\text{Area}=\frac12(13)\left(\frac{13\sin47^\circ}{\sin62^\circ}\right)\sin71^\circ=66.1788645053\ldots\text{ cm}^2.\]

    Use half the product of two sides times the sine of their included angle; retain unrounded values.

  4. Solution | Step 4
    \[\text{Area}=66.2\text{ cm}^2\quad\text{(3 significant figures)}.\]

    Round only the final area.

Final answer

\(66.2\text{ cm}^2\)

19. May 2023 P1HR · Q25

Hard6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. The two sides around the

    The two sides around the \(30^\circ\) angle are

    \[ 2x-1 \quad \text{and} \quad 2x+1 \]
  2. Use trigonometry

    Using the area formula,

    \[ \text{area}=\frac{1}{2}ab\sin C \]
    \[ x^2+x-3.75 =\frac{1}{2}(2x-1)(2x+1)\sin 30^\circ \]
  3. Use trigonometry

    Since \(\sin 30^\circ=\frac{1}{2}\),

    \[ x^2+x-3.75=\frac{1}{4}(2x-1)(2x+1) \]
    \[ x^2+x-3.75=\frac{1}{4}(4x^2-1) \]
    \[ x^2+x-3.75=x^2-0.25 \]
    \[ x=3.5 \]
  4. Use trigonometry

    So

    \[ AB=2x-1=6,\qquad AC=2x+1=8 \]
  5. Use cosine rule

    Use the cosine rule to find \(BC\):

    \[ BC^2=6^2+8^2-2(6)(8)\cos 30^\circ \]
    \[ BC=4.1063\ldots \]
  6. Use trigonometry

    The largest side is \(AC=8\), so the largest angle is \(\angle B\).

  7. Use trigonometry

    Using the cosine rule again:

    \[ \cos B=\frac{6^2+BC^2-8^2}{2(6)(BC)} \]
    \[ B=103.0643\ldots^\circ \]
Final answer

\(\boxed{103^\circ}\)

  1. Use trigonometry

    \[ PQ=1.6,\qquad PR=4.2,\qquad \angle PRQ=18^\circ \]

    Use the sine rule:

    \[ \frac{\sin Q}{PR}=\frac{\sin R}{PQ} \]
    \[ \frac{\sin Q}{4.2}=\frac{\sin18^\circ}{1.6} \]
    \[ \sin Q=\frac{4.2\sin18^\circ}{1.6} \]
    \[ \sin Q=0.811169\ldots \]
  3. Use trigonometry

    Since angle \(PQR\) is obtuse,

    \[ Q=180^\circ-\sin^{-1}(0.811169\ldots) \]
    \[ Q=125.7896\ldots^\circ \]
  4. Use trigonometry

    Now find angle \(P\):

    \[ P=180^\circ-18^\circ-125.7896\ldots^\circ \]
    \[ P=36.2103\ldots^\circ \]
  5. Area of triangle

    Area of triangle \(PQR\):

    \[ \frac12(PQ)(PR)\sin P \]
    \[ =\frac12(1.6)(4.2)\sin(36.2103\ldots^\circ) \]
    \[ =1.984925\ldots \]
  6. Correct to 3 significant figures

    Correct to 3 significant figures:

21. Jan 2020 P1HR · Q21

Hard6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use trigonometry

    In triangle \(BEC\), \(CE=13\) and \(\angle BEC=40^\circ\).

  2. Find the gradient

    Since this is a prism, \(BE\) is perpendicular to the cross section, so triangle \(BEC\) is right-angled at \(B\).

    \[ BC=13\sin40^\circ \]
    \[ BC=8.356238\ldots \]
  3. The area of triangle is

    The area of triangle \(ABC\) is \(22\text{ cm}^2\).

  4. Use trigonometry

    Using

    \[ \text{Area}=\frac12 ab\sin C \]
    \[ 22=\frac12(AC)(BC)\sin(\angle ACB) \]
    \[ 22=\frac12(6)(8.356238\ldots)\sin(\angle ACB) \]
    \[ \sin(\angle ACB)=0.877587\ldots \]
  5. Use trigonometry

    The angle \(ACB\) is obtuse, so

    \[ \angle ACB=180^\circ-\sin^{-1}(0.877587\ldots) \]
    \[ \angle ACB=118.647\ldots^\circ \]
  6. Use the cosine rule

    Use the cosine rule:

    \[ AB^2=AC^2+BC^2-2(AC)(BC)\cos(\angle ACB) \]
    \[ AB^2=6^2+(8.356238\ldots)^2-2(6)(8.356238\ldots)\cos(118.647\ldots^\circ) \]
    \[ AB=12.4056\ldots \]
  7. Correct to one decimal place

    Correct to one decimal place:

    \[ 12.4 \]
Final answer

\(\boxed{12.4\text{ cm}}\)
