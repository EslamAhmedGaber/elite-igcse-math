  1. Solve equation

    For part (a),

    \[ \frac{5a+8}{3}-\frac{2a+5}{4}=23 \]
  2. Multiply by

    Multiply by \(12\):

    \[ 4(5a+8)-3(2a+5)=276 \]
    \[ 20a+32-6a-15=276 \]

    \[ 14a=259 \]
    \[ a=\frac{259}{14}=\frac{37}{2} \]
  3. Solve equation

    For part (b),

    \[ \left(\frac{\sqrt y}{3}\right)^{-1}=\frac{3}{\sqrt y} \]

11. Jun 2026 P2HR · Q4

Hard8 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1
    \[3x+6=1-5x.\]

    Expand the bracket.

  2. Part (a) | Step 2
    \[8x=-5\quad\Rightarrow\quad x=-\frac58.\]

    Add 5x and subtract 6 on both sides, then divide by 8.

  3. Part (b) | Step 1
    \[P=(-7)^2-5(3)=49-15=34.\]

    The square applies to the whole negative number, so (-7) squared is positive.

  4. Part (c) | Step 1

    \(m^0=1\) for \(m\ne0\).

    Any nonzero base to the zero power equals 1.

  5. Part (d) | Step 1
    \[(4r^2t)^3=4^3(r^2)^3t^3=64r^6t^3.\]

    Cube each factor and multiply the exponents in the power of a power.

Final answer

(a) \(x=-\frac58\).

(b) \(P=34\).

(c) \(1\).

(d) \(64r^6t^3\).
