  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[(-4)(-8)=32,\qquad -4+(-8)=-12.\]

    Choose two numbers with product 32 and sum -12.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[x^2-12x+32=(x-4)(x-8).\]

    The chosen numbers give the two constant terms in the factors.

Final answer

\((x-4)(x-8)\)

18. Jun 2026 P2H · Q9

Hard7 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1

    Since \(n>0\), \(n^0=1\). Hence \(7n^0=7(1)=7\).

    A nonzero number raised to power zero equals 1; only n is raised to zero.

  2. Part (b) | Step 1
    \[(2m^5p^4)^3=2^3m^{5\times3}p^{4\times3}=8m^{15}p^{12}.\]

    Cube the coefficient and multiply each exponent by 3.

  3. Part (c) | Step 1

    The highest common factor of \(18c^5w^2\) and \(30cw^3\) is \(6cw^2\).

    Take the numerical HCF and the smallest power of each shared variable.

  4. Part (c) | Step 2
    \[18c^5w^2+30cw^3=6cw^2(3c^4+5w).\]

    Divide each original term by the common factor.

  5. Part (d) | Step 1

    Two numbers with product \(-27\) and sum \(6\) are \(9\) and \(-3\).

    For a monic quadratic, find a factor pair with the required sum.

  6. Part (d) | Step 2
    \[x^2+6x-27=(x+9)(x-3).\]

    The cross terms are -3x+9x=6x.

Final answer

(a) \(7\)

(b) \(8m^{15}p^{12}\)

(c) \(6cw^2(3c^4+5w)\)

(d) \((x+9)(x-3)\)
