  1. Substitute (brackets round the negative)
    \[ p^2-3q=2.6^2-3(-1.8)=6.76+5.4=12.16 \]
    \[ \sqrt{p+q^2}=\sqrt{2.6+(-1.8)^2}=\sqrt{5.84}=2.4166\ldots \]
  2. Divide and round
    \[ \frac{12.16}{2.4166\ldots}=5.03184\ldots\approx5.032 \]
Final answer

\(5.032\)
