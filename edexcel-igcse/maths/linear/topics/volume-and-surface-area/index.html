  1. Required

    Cuboid has a square cross-section of side *x* cm and length (15−4*x*) cm. Find the maximum value of the volume *V*.

  2. Write V as a function

    Write *V* as a function of *x*: *V* = *x* ⋅ *x* ⋅ (15−4*x*) = *x*<sup>2</sup>(15−4*x*) = 15*x*<sup>2</sup> − 4*x*<sup>3</sup>

  3. Differentiate

    Differentiate: $$\\frac{dV}{dx} = 30x - 12x^2$$

  4. Set ( x 0 gives

    Set $\\dfrac{dV}{dx} = 0$: $$30x - 12x^2 = 0 \\implies 6x(5-2x) = 0 \\implies x=0\\;\\text{ or }\\;x=\\tfrac{5}{2}=2.5$$ (*x* = 0 gives zero volume, so the meaningful solution is *x* = 2.5.)

  5. Second derivative test to confirm

    Second derivative test to confirm maximum: $$\\frac{d^2V}{dx^2} = 30-24x\\;\\Bigg\|\_{x=2.5} = 30 - 60 = -30 \< 0 \\quad \\checkmark\\text{ (maximum)}$$

  6. Calculate V max V max

    Calculate *V*<sub>max</sub>: *V*<sub>max</sub> = (2.5)<sup>2</sup> × (15−4×2.5) = 6.25 × 5 = 31.25 cm<sup>3</sup>

  7. Note

    Calculus optimisation: always verify the nature of the stationary point using $\\dfrac{d^2V}{dx^2}$. Also check *x* \> 0 and 15 − 4*x* \> 0 (i.e. *x* \< 3.75) to ensure a valid cuboid.

Final answer

Maximum volume *V* = 31.25 cm<sup>3</sup>

17. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q25

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    Hemisphere H has radius *x* cm and volume 6174*π* cm<sup>3</sup>. A bowl is made by removing an inner hemisphere, leaving 2 cm thickness. Find total surface area in terms of *π*.

  2. Calculate volume

    Find outer radius *x*. Volume of hemisphere $= \\dfrac{2}{3}\\pi r^3$: $$\\frac{2}{3}\\pi x^3 = 6174\\pi \\implies x^3 = \\frac{6174\\times3}{2} = 9261 \\implies x = \\sqrt\[3\]{9261} = 21\\text{ cm}$$

  3. Find inner radius r inner

    Find inner radius: *r*<sub>inner</sub> = 21 − 2 = 19 cm

  4. Add 1

    Three surfaces to add:

    **1. Outer curved surface** (half-sphere, radius 21): *A*<sub>1</sub> = 2*π*(21)<sup>2</sup> = 2*π* × 441 = 882*π*

    **2. Inner curved surface** (half-sphere, radius 19): *A*<sub>2</sub> = 2*π*(19)<sup>2</sup> = 2*π* × 361 = 722*π*

    **3. Flat annular ring** at the top rim: *A*<sub>3</sub> = *π*(21)<sup>2</sup> − *π*(19)<sup>2</sup> = *π*(441−361) = 80*π*

  5. Total surface area A total

    Total surface area: *A*<sub>total</sub> = 882*π* + 722*π* + 80*π* = 1684*π*

  6. Note

    Three separate surfaces! Missing the flat annular ring is the most common error on this type of question.

Final answer

Total surface area  = 1684*π* cm<sup>2</sup>
