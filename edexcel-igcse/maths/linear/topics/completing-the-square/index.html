14. Jun 2026 P1H · Q24

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1
    \[10-24x-3x^2=10-3(x^2+8x).\]

    Factor -3 from the terms involving x.

  2. Part (a) | Step 2
    \[x^2+8x=(x+4)^2-16.\]

    Half the coefficient of x is 4.

  3. Part (a) | Step 3
    \[10-3\big((x+4)^2-16\big)=58-3(x+4)^2.\]

    Thus a=58, b=3 and c=4.

  4. Part (b) | Step 1
    \[y=58-3\big((x-1)+4\big)^2=58-3(x+3)^2.\]

    Use (a) with x replaced throughout by x-1.

  5. Part (b) | Step 2

    Since \((x+3)^2\ge0\), the greatest y is 58, attained at \(x=-3\). Thus \(P=(-3,58)\).

    The negative square term is largest when its square is zero.

  6. Part (c) | Step 1

    The parabola is symmetric about its maximum, so its line of symmetry is \(x=-3\).

    In 58-3(x+3)^2, equal positive and negative offsets from -3 give the same y.

Final answer

(a) \(58-3(x+4)^2\)

(b) \(P=(-3,58)\)

(c) \(x=-3\)
