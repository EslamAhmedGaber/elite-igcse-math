1. Nov 2025 4WM2H · Q1

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Construct angle bisector

    **Step 1:** Place the point of the compass at the vertex *Q*. Draw an arc that intersects both arms *Q**P* and *Q**R*. Let’s call these intersection points *A* and *B*. **Step 2:** Without changing the compass width, place the compass point on *A* and draw an arc inside the angle. **Step 3:** Place the compass point on *B* and draw another arc to intersect the previous arc. Let this intersection be point *C*. **Step 4:** Draw a straight line from *Q* through *C*. This is the angle bisector.

Final answer

This is the angle bisector.

2. Nov 2025 P1H · Q3

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Construction answer

    To construct the bisector of angle \(ABC\):

    1. Use centre \(B\) to draw an arc crossing \(BA\) and \(BC\). 2. Use the same compass width from the two crossing points to make two arcs inside the angle. 3. Join \(B\) to the point where these two arcs meet.

Final answer

That line is the required angle bisector.

3. Jun 2025 P2H · Q4

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Construction answer

    To construct the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\):

    1. Open the compass to more than half the length of \(AB\). 2. Draw equal arcs from \(A\) above and below \(AB\). 3. Without changing the compass width, draw equal arcs from \(B\). 4. Join the two arc intersections with a ruler.

Final answer

The joined line is the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).

  1. Construction answer

    To construct the bisector of angle \(ABC\):

    1. Draw an arc centred at \(B\) cutting both arms of the angle. 2. From the two cut points, draw equal arcs inside the angle. 3. Draw the line from \(B\) through the intersection of these arcs.

Final answer

The constructed ray is the angle bisector of \(ABC\).

7. Jan 2020 P2H · Q5

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Construction answer

    To construct the bisector of angle \(BAC\):

    1. Put the compass point at \(A\) and draw an arc that cuts \(AB\) and \(AC\). 2. From the two points where the arc cuts the arms, draw equal arcs inside the angle. 3. Draw a straight line from \(A\) through the intersection of those two arcs.

Final answer

The ray from \(A\) through the arc intersection is the angle bisector of \(BAC\).

  1. Construction answer

    To construct the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\):

    1. Set the compass radius to more than half of \(AB\). 2. With centre \(A\), draw arcs above and below the line. 3. With the same radius and centre \(B\), draw arcs crossing the first pair. 4. Draw the straight line through the two arc intersections.

Final answer

This line is the perpendicular bisector of \(AB\).

10. Jan 2022 P2H · Q5

  1. The distance from to is

    The distance from \(A\) to \(C\) is \(700\text{ m}\).

  2. The scale is
    \[ 1\text{ cm represents }200\text{ m} \]
  3. Use bearings

    So the map distance is \[ 700\div 200=3.5\text{ cm} \]

  4. Use bearings

    Part (a): from \(A\), draw a bearing of \(110^\circ\) and mark \(C\) at \(3.5\text{ cm}\) from \(A\).

  5. Use bearings

    For part (b), \[ 200\text{ m}=20000\text{ cm} \]

  6. Use bearings

    So the scale is \[ 1:20000 \]

Final answer

Draw \(C\) 3.5 cm from \(A\) on a bearing of \(110^\circ\), and the scale is \(1:20000\).

11. Nov 2023 P1H · Q18

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. The bearing of from is

    The bearing of \(B\) from \(A\) is \(054^\circ\), so the bearing of \(A\) from \(B\) is \[ 054^\circ+180^\circ=234^\circ \]

  2. The bearing of from is

    The bearing of \(C\) from \(B\) is \(132^\circ\).

  3. Use bearings

    So \[ \angle ABC=234^\circ-132^\circ=102^\circ \]

  4. Use cosine rule

    Use the cosine rule to find \(AC\): \[ AC^2=3.6^2+8.4^2-2(3.6)(8.4)\cos 102^\circ \]

    \[ AC=9.8028\ldots\text{ km} \]
  5. Total distance walked

    Total distance walked: \[ 3.6+8.4+9.8028\ldots=21.8028\ldots\text{ km} \]

  6. Use bearings

    At \(6\text{ km/h}\), time is \[ 21.8028\ldots\div 6=3.6338\ldots\text{ hours} \]

    \[ 0.6338\ldots\times 60=38.0\ldots\text{ minutes} \]
Final answer

\(3\) hours \(38\) minutes.

12. Jan 2021 P1H · Q16

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use bearings

    From \(A\), ship \(C\) is on a bearing of \(120^\circ\), and \(AC=275\text{ m}\).

  2. Resolve

    Resolve \(AC\): \[ \text{east component}=275\sin 120^\circ=238.16\ldots \] \[ \text{north component}=275\cos 120^\circ=-137.5 \]

  3. Use bearings

    Ship \(B\) is \(150\text{ m}\) north of \(A\), so from \(B\) to \(C\): \[ \text{east}=238.16\ldots,\qquad \text{north}=-137.5-150=-287.5 \]

  4. Use bearings

    The bearing is therefore in the south-east quadrant: \[ 180^\circ-\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{238.16\ldots}{287.5}\right)=140.36\ldots^\circ \]

Final answer

\(140^\circ\).

13. Nov 2024 P2H · Q24

Hard6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Resolve each journey into east

    Resolve each journey into east and north components.

  2. Use trigonometry

    From \(A\) to \(B\):

    \[ \text{east}=8.4\sin 67^\circ=7.7322\ldots \]
    \[ \text{north}=8.4\cos 67^\circ=3.2821\ldots \]
  3. Use trigonometry

    From \(B\) to \(C\):

    \[ \text{east}=9.2\sin 129^\circ=7.1497\ldots \]
    \[ \text{north}=9.2\cos 129^\circ=-5.7897\ldots \]
  4. Calculate value

    So from \(A\) to \(C\):

    \[ \text{east}=14.8820\ldots \]
    \[ \text{north}=-2.5076\ldots \]
  5. Calculate value

    Therefore from \(C\) to \(A\):

    \[ \text{east}=-14.8820\ldots,\qquad \text{north}=2.5076\ldots \]
  6. Use trigonometry

    This direction is west of north.

    \[ \theta=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{14.8820\ldots}{2.5076\ldots}\right) =80.4355\ldots^\circ \]
  7. Use bearings

    Bearing of \(A\) from \(C\):

    \[ 360^\circ-80.4355\ldots^\circ=279.5644\ldots^\circ \]
Final answer

\(\boxed{280^\circ}\)
