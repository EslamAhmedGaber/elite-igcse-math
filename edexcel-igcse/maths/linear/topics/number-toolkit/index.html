  1. (a) Each number is ten times smaller

    \(2.3\times4.7=1081\div100=10.81\)

  2. (b) Reverse the multiplication

    \(1081\div47=23\), and \(4.7\) is ten times smaller than \(47\), so \(1081\div4.7=230\).

Final answer

(a) \(10.81\) (b) \(230\)

2. Jun 2026 P2H · Q1

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[4500=45\times100=(9\times5)(4\times25).\]

    Split into factors that are easy to prime-factorise.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[4500=(3^2\times5)(2^2\times5^2)=2^2\times3^2\times5^3.\]

    Express each factor as prime powers and combine powers of 5.

Final answer

\(2^2\times3^2\times5^3\)

3. Jun 2026 P1HR · Q2

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[504=2\times252=2^2\times126=2^3\times63.\]

    Divide repeatedly by the prime 2.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[63=3\times21=3^2\times7,\qquad 504=2^3\times3^2\times7.\]

    All remaining factors are prime; group repeated factors as powers.

Final answer

\(504=2^3\times3^2\times7\)

  1. (a) Exactly two factors
