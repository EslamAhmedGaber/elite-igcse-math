9. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q2

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    Write 1400 as a product of powers of its prime factors.

  2. Divide repeatedly by the smallest

    Divide repeatedly by the smallest prime factor each time: $$1400 \\;\\xrightarrow{\\div 2}\\; 700 \\;\\xrightarrow{\\div 2}\\; 350 \\;\\xrightarrow{\\div 2}\\; 175 \\;\\xrightarrow{\\div 5}\\; 35 \\;\\xrightarrow{\\div 5}\\; 7 \\;\\xrightarrow{\\div 7}\\; 1$$

  3. Collect the prime factors

    Collect the prime factors: $$1400 = \\underbrace{2 \\times 2 \\times 2}\_{2^3} \\times \\underbrace{5 \\times 5}\_{5^2} \\times 7$$

Final answer

1400 = 2<sup>3</sup> × 5<sup>2</sup> × 7
