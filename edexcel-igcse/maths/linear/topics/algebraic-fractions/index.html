  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[4x^2-9=(2x-3)(2x+3),\qquad2x^2-5x-12=(2x+3)(x-4).\]

    Use difference of squares for the numerator and factorise the denominator.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[\frac{4x^2-9}{2x^2-5x-12}=\frac{(2x-3)(2x+3)}{(2x+3)(x-4)}=\frac{2x-3}{x-4}.\]

    Cancel the common factor, not individual terms.

  3. Solution | Step 3

    The original fraction requires \(x\ne-\frac32\) and \(x\ne4\).

    Cancellation does not restore excluded values.

Final answer

\(\frac{2x-3}{x-4}\), \(x\ne-\frac32,4\).

    Multiply each term in the first bracket by each term in the second.

  4. Part (b) | Step 2
    \[3x(2x^2+7x-4)=6x^3+21x^2-12x.\]

    Multiply every term by 3x.

Final answer

(a) \(\frac{3x-4}{8x}\)

(b) \(6x^3+21x^2-12x\)
