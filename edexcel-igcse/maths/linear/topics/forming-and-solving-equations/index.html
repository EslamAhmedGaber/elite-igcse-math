1. Nov 2025 P2H · Q20

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Calculate value

    For

    \[ ax^2+4x+c=0 \]
  2. the quadratic formula gives
    \[ x=\frac{-4\pm \sqrt{4^2-4ac}}{2a} \]
  3. The given solutions are
    \[ x=\frac{-4\pm 2\sqrt{39}}{10} \]
  4. Compare denominators

    Compare denominators:

    \[ 2a=10 \]
    \[ a=5 \]
  5. Compare the square root parts

    Compare the square root parts:

    \[ \sqrt{16-4ac}=2\sqrt{39} \]
  6. Square both sides

    Square both sides:

    \[ 16-4ac=156 \]
  7. Use

    Use \(a=5\):

    \[ 16-20c=156 \]
    \[ -20c=140 \]
    \[ c=-7 \]
Final answer

\(a=5,\ c=-7\)

  2. Form and solve the equation

5. Nov 2021 P1H · Q5

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. The top row has 5

    The top row has 5 tile widths and the bottom row has 4 tile lengths, so

    \[ 5W=4L \]
    \[ L=\frac54W \]
  2. Use trigonometry

    The rectangle has width \(5W\) and height \(L+W\):

    \[ 5W(L+W)=1620 \]
  3. Substitute

    Substitute \(L=\frac54W\):

    \[ 5W\left(\frac54W+W\right)=1620 \]
    \[ 5W\left(\frac94W\right)=1620 \]
    \[ \frac{45}{4}W^2=1620 \]
    \[ W^2=144 \]
    \[ W=12,\qquad L=\frac54(12)=15 \]
Final answer

\(L=15\text{ cm}\), \(W=12\text{ cm}\).
