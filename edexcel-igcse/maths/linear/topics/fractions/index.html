1. Jun 2026 P2HR · Q17

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1

    Let \(z=0.\overline{814}=0.814814814\ldots\). Then \(1000z=814.814814\ldots\).

    Multiplication by 1000 aligns the repeating three-digit tails.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[1000z-z=814\quad\Rightarrow\quad999z=814\quad\Rightarrow\quad z=\frac{814}{999}=\frac{22}{27}.\]

    Subtract to cancel the recurring tails, then cancel the common factor 37.

Final answer

\(0.\overline{814}=\frac{22}{27}\).

17. Nov 2025 P2H · Q3

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. There are red and blue

    There are \(9+43=52\) red and blue bricks.

  2. Simplify fraction

    Let the total number of bricks be \(T\).

  3. Simplify fraction

    Since \(\frac7{20}\) of the bricks are yellow, the remaining fraction is

    \[ 1-\frac7{20}=\frac{13}{20} \]
  4. Simplify fraction

    So

    \[ \frac{13}{20}T=52 \]
    \[ T=52\times \frac{20}{13}=80 \]
  5. Yellow bricks

    Yellow bricks:

    \[ \frac7{20}\times 80=28 \]
  6. Simplify fraction

    Each brick weighs \(35\) grams, so total yellow-brick weight is

    \[ 28\times 35=980 \]
Final answer

\(980\) grams
