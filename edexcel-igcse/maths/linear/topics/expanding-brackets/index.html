11. Jun 2026 P1H · Q12

Hard6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1
    \[\frac{2^{x/3}}{8^x}=\frac{2^{x/3}}{2^{3x}}=2^{x/3-3x}=2^{-8x/3}.\]

    Write 8 as a power of 2 and subtract exponents when dividing.

  2. Part (a) | Step 2
    \[2^{-8x/3}=64=2^6\implies-\frac{8x}{3}=6\implies x=-\frac94.\]

    Equal powers of base 2 have equal exponents.

  3. Part (b) | Step 1
    \[(t+5)(2t-3)=2t^2-3t+10t-15=2t^2+7t-15.\]

    Expand the two brackets and collect like terms.

  4. Part (b) | Step 2
    \[4t(t+5)(2t-3)=4t(2t^2+7t-15)=8t^3+28t^2-60t.\]

    Multiply every term by 4t.

Final answer

(a) \(x=-\frac94\)

(b) \(8t^3+28t^2-60t\)

14. May 2025 4WM1H · Q11

Hard6 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Expand brackets
    \[ 5x(5x+4)(2x-3) \]
  2. Expand brackets

    First,

    \[ (5x+4)(2x-3)=10x^2-15x+8x-12=10x^2-7x-12 \]
  3. Expand brackets

    Then multiply by \(5x\).

    \[ 5x(10x^2-7x-12)=50x^3-35x^2-60x \]
  4. Expand brackets

    For part (b), use denominator \(20y\).

    \[ \frac94+\frac{y-7}{5y}=\frac{45y}{20y}+\frac{4(y-7)}{20y} \]
    \[ =\frac{49y-28}{20y} \]
Final answer

\(50x^3-35x^2-60x\) and \(\frac{49y-28}{20y}\).
