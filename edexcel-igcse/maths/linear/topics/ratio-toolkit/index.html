1. Nov 2023 P2H · Q5

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Butter sugar

    Butter : sugar \(=3:2\).

  2. Split the ratio

    Sugar is \(120\) g, so

    \[ 2\text{ parts}=120 \]
    \[ 1\text{ part}=60 \]
    \[ \text{butter}=3\times 60=180 \]
  3. Flour butter

    Flour : butter \(=6:5\).

    \[ 5\text{ parts}=180 \]
    \[ 1\text{ part}=36 \]
    \[ \text{flour}=6\times 36=216 \]
Final answer

\(216\) grams

  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[\text{Sarah to B}=400\times\frac{3}{2+3}=240\text{ dollars}.\]

    Charity B receives 3 of the 5 equal shares.

  2. Solution | Step 2

    Dej gives to A and B in the ratio \(1:2\), so \[\text{Dej to B}=375\times\frac{2}{3}=250\text{ dollars}.\]

  1. Split the ratio

    \[ \text{girls}=120\times \frac38=45 \]
    \[ \text{boys}=120\times \frac58=75 \]
  2. Split the ratio

    Boys climbing:

    \[ \frac{16}{25}\times 75=48 \]
  3. Split the ratio

    Boys sailing:

    \[ 75-48=27 \]

    \[ \text{sailing}:\text{climbing}=2:1 \]
  5. Split the ratio

    Girls sailing:

    \[ \frac23\times 45=30 \]
  6. Total sailing

    Total sailing:

    \[ 27+30=57 \]
Final answer

\(57\)
