33. Jun 2026 P1H · Q20

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[99.5\le s<100.5,\quad1.675\le t<1.685,\quad0.45\le u<0.55.\]

    Use half a rounding unit on each side of the stated value.

  2. Solution | Step 2

    For \(v=2s/t-u\), the lower limiting value is \[v_L=\frac{2(99.5)}{1.685}-0.55=117.550890\ldots\text{ m/s}.\]

    Decrease the numerator, increase the positive denominator and subtract the largest u.

  3. Solution | Step 3

    The upper limiting value is \[v_U=\frac{2(100.5)}{1.675}-0.45=119.55\text{ m/s}.\]

    Increase the numerator, decrease the denominator and subtract the smallest u.

  4. Solution | Step 4

    Both limiting values round to \(120\text{ m/s}\) to 2 significant figures. They do not round to the same integer, so \[v=120\text{ m/s}\quad(2\text{ s.f.}).\]

    This is the greatest common significant-figure accuracy justified throughout the interval.

Final answer

\(120\text{ m/s}\) to 2 significant figures.
