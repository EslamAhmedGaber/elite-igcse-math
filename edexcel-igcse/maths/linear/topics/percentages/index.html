  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[\left(1+\frac{x}{100}\right)\left(1+\frac{2x}{100}\right)=1.3888.\]

    The second increase acts on the already-increased house value.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[1+\frac{3x}{100}+\frac{2x^2}{10000}=1.3888\quad\Rightarrow\quad x^2+150x-1944=0.\]

    Expand, subtract 1, and multiply by 5000.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[(x-12)(x+162)=0\quad\Rightarrow\quad x=12\text{ or }x=-162.\]

    Factorize the quadratic.

  4. Solution | Step 4

    The positive root gives multipliers 1.12 and 1.24, whose product is 1.3888.

\(x=12\).
