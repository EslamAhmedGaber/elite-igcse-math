  1. (a) Collect like terms
    \[ 5a-2a=3a,\qquad 3b+7b=10b \;\Rightarrow\; 3a+10b \]
  2. (b) Multiply the numbers, then the letters
    \[ 4\times3=12 \;\Rightarrow\; 12xy \]
Final answer

(a) \(3a+10b\) (b) \(12xy\)

8. Elite Practice · Q29

Easy2 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Substitute n = −2 into each expression
    \[ 3n=-6,\quad n+3=1,\quad n^3=-8,\quad 3n^2=3\times4=12,\quad -n=2 \]
  2. Pick the greatest

    \(3n^2=12\) is the greatest.

Final answer

\(3n^2\) (value \(12\))
