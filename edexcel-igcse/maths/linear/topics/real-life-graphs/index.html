1. Elite Practice · Q12

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Distance = area under the graph

    Triangle: \(\tfrac12\times T\times12=6T\). Rectangle: \(30\times12=360\).

  2. Form and solve the equation
    \[ 6T+360=450 \;\Rightarrow\; 6T=90 \;\Rightarrow\; T=15 \]
Final answer

\(T=15\)

2. Elite Practice · Q8

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Read the graph at 10 minutes

    The line from \((0,0)\) to \((20,1.6)\) is halfway up at \(10\) minutes: \(0.8\) km.

  2. (b) The flat section

    From \(20\) to \(30\) minutes: \(10\) minutes.

  3. (c) Speed = distance ÷ time

    He walks \(1.6\) km in \(50-30=20\) minutes \(=\frac13\) hour.

    \[ 1.6\div\frac13=4.8\text{ km/h} \]
Final answer

(a) \(0.8\) km (b) \(10\) minutes (c) \(4.8\) km/h

3. Elite Practice · Q10

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Area of the trapezium (first 10 s)
    \[ \frac{4+16}{2}\times10=100\text{ m} \]
  2. (a) Area of the rectangle (last 15 s)
    \[ 16\times15=240\text{ m} \]

    Total: \(100+240=340\) m.

  3. (b) Average speed = total distance ÷ total time
    \[ 340\div25=13.6\text{ m/s} \]
Final answer

(a) \(340\) m (b) \(13.6\) m/s

4. Elite Practice · Q11

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Where the lines cross

    The lines cross at \(36\) minutes after 10:00, so at \(10{:}36\).

  2. (b) Read across

    At the crossing point the distance from \(A\) is \(9\) km.

  3. (c) Tom's speed

    Tom goes from \(12\) km to \(7\) km from \(A\) in \(1\) hour:

    \[ \frac{12-7}{1}=5\text{ km/h} \]
Final answer

(a) \(10{:}36\) (b) \(9\) km (c) \(5\) km/h

5. Elite Practice · Q13

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Gradient of the line
    \[ \frac{5-20}{6}=-2.5 \]

    So the deceleration is \(2.5\) m/s\(^2\).

  2. (b) Area of the trapezium
    \[ \frac{20+5}{2}\times6=75\text{ m} \]
Final answer

(a) \(2.5\) m/s\(^2\) (b) \(75\) m

6. Elite Practice · Q14

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Sketch the speed-time graph

    A trapezium: up for \(50\) s, flat at \(30\) m/s for \(t\) s, down for \(40\) s.

  2. Distance = area (in metres)
    \[ \tfrac12\times50\times30+30t+\tfrac12\times40\times30=750+30t+600 \]
  3. Form and solve the equation

    \(6\) km \(=6000\) m:

    \[ 1350+30t=6000 \;\Rightarrow\; 30t=4650 \;\Rightarrow\; t=155 \]
Final answer

\(t=155\)

7. Elite Practice · Q9

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Acceleration = gradient
    \[ \frac{18-0}{12}=1.5\text{ m/s}^2 \]
  2. (b) Distance = area under the graph

    Split into a triangle, a rectangle and a triangle:

    \[ \tfrac12\times12\times18+20\times18+\tfrac12\times8\times18=108+360+72=540\text{ m} \]
Final answer

(a) \(1.5\) m/s\(^2\) (b) \(540\) m
