  2. O A B is isosceles

    △*O**B**C* is isosceles (*O**B* = *O**C*): ∠*O**B**C* = ∠*O**C**B* = 21° ⟹ ∠*B**O**C* = 180° − 21° − 21° = 138°

  4. Angles at centre O (on

    Angles at centre O (on the same side): ∠*A**O**C* = 360° − 108° − 138° = 114°

  5. O A C is isosceles

    △*O**A**C* is isosceles (*O**A* = *O**C*): $$\\angle OAC + \\angle OCA = 180°-114° = 66° \\implies \\angle ACO = \\frac{66°}{2} = 33°$$

6. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q18

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    DB is a diameter; chords AC and DB intersect at P. AP = 12 cm, PC = 7 cm, PB = 6 cm. Find area of circle (3 s.f.).

  2. Apply the Intersecting Chords Theorem

    Apply the **Intersecting Chords Theorem**: *A**P* × *P**C* = *B**P* × *P**D* $$12\\times7 = 6\\times PD \\implies PD = \\frac{84}{6} = 14\\text{ cm}$$

  3. Diameter D B D B

    Diameter *D**B*: *D**B* = *P**D* + *P**B* = 14 + 6 = 20 cm ⟹ *r* = 10 cm

  4. Area of circle Area r

    Area of circle: Area = *π**r*<sup>2</sup> = 100*π* ≈ 314.159… cm<sup>2</sup>

  5. Note

    Intersecting Chords Theorem: if two chords cross inside a circle at P, then *A**P* × *P**C* = *B**P* × *P**D*.

Final answer

Area of circle  = 314 cm<sup>2</sup> (3 s.f.)

    By the alternate segment theorem, the angle between tangent \(BC\) and chord \(BF\) equals the angle in the opposite segment: \[ \angle BEF=70^\circ \]

    Angles \(EDF\) and \(EBF\) stand on the same chord \(EF\), so \[ \angle EBF=40^\circ \]
