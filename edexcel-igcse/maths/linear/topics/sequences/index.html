  1. Find common difference

    **Step 1: Find the common difference *d*.** The *n*<sup>th</sup> term is given by *u*<sub>*n*</sub> = *a* + (*n*−1)*d*. *u*<sub>20</sub> = 10 + 19*d* = 86 19*d* = 76 $$d = \\frac{76}{19} = 4$$

  2. Use sum formula

    **Step 2: Use the sum formula to form an equation in terms of *N*.** $$S_N = \\frac{N}{2} \\left\[2a + (N - 1)d \\right\]$$ Substitute the known values *S*<sub>*N*</sub> = 5194, *a* = 10, and *d* = 4: $$5194 = \\frac{N}{2} \\left\[2(10) + (N - 1)(4) \\right\]$$

  3. Solve quadratic equation

    **Step 3: Simplify and solve the quadratic equation.** Multiply by 2 to clear the fraction: 10388 = *N*\[20+4*N*−4\] 10388 = *N*(4*N*+16) 4*N*<sup>2</sup> + 16*N* − 10388 = 0

  4. Divide the entire equation by

    Divide the entire equation by 4 to make it simpler: *N*<sup>2</sup> + 4*N* − 2597 = 0

  5. Use quadratic formula

    **Step 4: Use the quadratic formula to find *N*.** $$N = \\frac{-b \\pm \\sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$ $$N = \\frac{-4 \\pm \\sqrt{16 - 4(1)(-2597)}}{2}$$ $$N = \\frac{-4 \\pm \\sqrt{16 + 10388}}{2}$$ $$N = \\frac{-4 \\pm \\sqrt{10404}}{2}$$ $$N = \\frac{-4 \\pm 102}{2}$$

  6. Evaluate fraction

    Since *N* must be a positive integer, we discard the negative root: $$N = \\frac{-4 + 102}{2} = \\frac{98}{2} = 49$$

Final answer
