1. Jun 2026 P2H · Q14

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Part (a) | Step 1

    The median is the \(60/2=30\)th value. Draw horizontally from cumulative frequency \(30\) to the curve, then vertically to the weight axis: about \(117\text{ kg}\).

    The median splits the 60 observations in half.

  2. Part (b) | Step 1

    Read the lower quartile at cumulative frequency \(60/4=15\), giving \(Q_1\approx112\text{ kg}\). Read the upper quartile at cumulative frequency \(3(60)/4=45\), giving \(Q_3\approx128\text{ kg}\).

    Quartiles occur at one-quarter and three-quarters of the total frequency.

  3. Part (b) | Step 2
    \[\text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1\approx128-112=16\text{ kg}.\]

    Interquartile range is upper quartile minus lower quartile.

Final answer

(a) \(117\text{ kg}\) approximately.

(b) \(16\text{ kg}\) approximately.
