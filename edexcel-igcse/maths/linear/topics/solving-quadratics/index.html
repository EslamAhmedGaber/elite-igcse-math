1. May 2025 4WM1H · Q15

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solve quadratic equation
    \[ 5x^2+9x-17=0 \]
  2. Use quadratic formula

    Use the quadratic formula with \(a=5\), \(b=9\), \(c=-17\).

    \[ x=\frac{-9\pm\sqrt{9^2-4(5)(-17)}}{2(5)} \]
    \[ x=\frac{-9\pm\sqrt{421}}{10} \]
    \[ x=1.152\ldots\quad\text{or}\quad x=-2.952\ldots \]
  3. Solve quadratic equation

    To 3 significant figures,

    \[ x=1.15\quad\text{or}\quad x=-2.95 \]
Final answer

\(x=1.15\) or \(x=-2.95\).

4. Elite Practice · Q30

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Identify a, b and c

    \(a=2,\ b=-5,\ c=-4\)

  2. Substitute into the formula
    \[ x=\frac{-(-5)\pm\sqrt{(-5)^2-4(2)(-4)}}{2(2)}=\frac{5\pm\sqrt{57}}{4} \]
  3. Work out both solutions
    \[ x=\frac{5+7.5498\ldots}{4}=3.14,\qquad x=\frac{5-7.5498\ldots}{4}=-0.637 \]
Final answer

\(x=3.14\) or \(x=-0.637\)

5. Elite Practice · Q31

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Use the formula
    \[ x=\frac{-6\pm\sqrt{36-8}}{2}=\frac{-6\pm\sqrt{28}}{2} \]
  2. Simplify the surd

    \(\sqrt{28}=2\sqrt7\), so

    \[ x=\frac{-6\pm2\sqrt7}{2}=-3\pm\sqrt7 \]
Final answer

\(x=-3\pm\sqrt7\)

6. Elite Practice · Q32

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. (a) Area = length × width
    \[ (x+3)(x-1)=20 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2+2x-3=20 \;\Rightarrow\; x^2+2x-23=0 \]
  2. (b) Use the formula
    \[ x=\frac{-2\pm\sqrt{4+92}}{2}=\frac{-2\pm\sqrt{96}}{2}=-1\pm\sqrt{24} \]

    The width \(x-1\) must be positive, so \(x=-1+\sqrt{24}=3.898\ldots\)

  3. (b) Length
    \[ x+3=6.898\ldots\approx6.90\text{ cm} \]
Final answer

(b) \(6.90\) cm
