8. May 2025 4WM2HR · Q12

Medium3 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Required

    Car bought for $16 000; depreciates 12%/yr for 2 yrs, then *x*%/yr in year 3. Value after 3 years  = $11 461.12. Find *x*.

  2. Value after 2 years at

    Value after 2 years at 12% depreciation: *V*<sub>2</sub> = 16000 × (0.88)<sup>2</sup> = 16000 × 0.7744 = $12 390.40

  3. Use compound interest

    Apply year-3 depreciation and set equal to final value: $$12390.40\\times\\!\\left(1 - \\frac{x}{100}\\right) = 11461.12$$

  4. Solve

    Solve: $$1 - \\frac{x}{100} = \\frac{11461.12}{12390.40} = 0.925 \\implies \\frac{x}{100} = 0.075 \\implies x = 7.5$$

Final answer

*x* = 7.5
