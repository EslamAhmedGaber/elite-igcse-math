5. Jun 2025 P2H · Q24

Medium4 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Let
    \[ AB=s \]
  2. Find the midpoint

    Since \(M\) is the centre of the square base and \(Q\) is the midpoint of \(AB\),

    \[ QM=\frac{s}{2} \]
  3. Use trigonometry

    Since \(EA=EB=EC=ED\), the point \(E\) is vertically above \(M\).

  4. Use trigonometry

    In right triangle \(EQM\),

    \[ \tan80^\circ=\frac{EM}{QM} \]
    \[ EM=\frac{s}{2}\tan80^\circ \]
  5. The distance from the centre

    The distance from the centre of a square to a vertex is half the diagonal:

    \[ AM=\frac{s\sqrt2}{2} \]
  6. Use trigonometry

    In right triangle \(EMA\),

    \[ EA^2=EM^2+AM^2 \]
    \[ EA^2=\left(\frac{s}{2}\tan80^\circ\right)^2+\left(\frac{s\sqrt2}{2}\right)^2 \]
    \[ EA=s\sqrt{\frac{\tan^2 80^\circ}{4}+\frac12} \]
  7. Use trigonometry

    So

    \[ \frac{EA}{AB}=\sqrt{\frac{\tan^2 80^\circ}{4}+\frac12} \]
    \[ n=2.92 \]
Final answer

\(n=2.92\)

11. Jun 2026 P1H · Q25

Hard5 marks
Show worked solution
  1. Solution | Step 1
    \[BF=\frac{12}{\sin30^\circ}=24\text{ cm},\qquad AB=\frac{12}{\tan30^\circ}=12\sqrt3\text{ cm}.\]

    Use right triangle ABF.

  2. Solution | Step 2
    \[BT=\frac23 BF=16\text{ cm}.\]

    Apply the given BF:BT ratio.

  3. Solution | Step 3
    \[AT=\sqrt{AB^2+BT^2}=\sqrt{432+256}=\sqrt{688}=4\sqrt{43}\text{ cm}.\]

    AB is perpendicular to BC in the rectangular base, so triangle ABT is right-angled at B.

  4. Solution | Step 4

    The projection of FT onto plane ABCD is AT because F lies vertically above A. Therefore the required angle is \(\theta=\angle FTA\).

    A line-plane angle is the angle between the line and its perpendicular projection onto the plane.

  5. Solution | Step 5
    \[\tan\theta=\frac{AF}{AT}=\frac{12}{4\sqrt{43}}=\frac{3}{\sqrt{43}},\quad\theta=24.583891\ldots^\circ=24.6^\circ.\]

    Use right triangle FAT and round to one decimal place.

Final answer

\(24.6^\circ\)

  1. The horizontal base is a

    The horizontal base \(ABGF\) is a rectangle.

    \[ AB=10,\qquad BG=14 \]
  2. Use trigonometry

    So the horizontal distance from \(B\) to \(F\) is

    \[ BF=\sqrt{10^2+14^2}=\sqrt{296} \]

    The angle of elevation of \(E\) from \(B\) is \(50^\circ\).

  4. Use trigonometry

    Since \(E\) is vertically above \(F\),

    \[ \tan 50^\circ=\frac{FE}{BF} \]
    \[ FE=\sqrt{296}\tan50^\circ \]

    The prism has the same cross section throughout, so

    \[ AD=FE=\sqrt{296}\tan50^\circ \]
  6. Use trigonometry

    Also

    \[ BC=\frac15AD \]
  7. Area of the trapezium cross

    Area of the trapezium cross section \(ABCD\):

    \[ \frac12(AD+BC)\times AB \]
    \[ =\frac12\left(AD+\frac15AD\right)\times 10 \]
    \[ =6AD \]
  8. Volume of the prism

    Volume of the prism:

    \[ 6AD\times 14=84AD \]
    \[ =84\sqrt{296}\tan50^\circ \]
    \[ =1722.311\ldots \]

    Correct to 3 significant figures:

    \[ 1720 \]

15. Jan 2022 P1H · Q21

  1. Let
    \[ AE=x \]
  2. Use trigonometry

    Since \(ABDE\) is a square,

    \[ AB=BD=DE=AE=x \]
  3. Use trigonometry

    Since \(BCD\) is an equilateral triangle, the height from \(C\) to \(BD\) is

    \[ \frac{\sqrt3}{2}x \]
  4. Use trigonometry

    Therefore the vertical height from the base to \(J\) is

    \[ JM=x+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}x \]
    \[ JM=\frac{x(2+\sqrt3)}{2} \]
  5. Use trigonometry

    Also,

    \[ EF=2AE=2x \]
  6. Find the midpoint

    Since \(M\) is the midpoint of \(GF\), the horizontal distance from \(A\) to \(M\) is

    \[ AM=\sqrt{\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)^2+(2x)^2} \]
    \[ AM=\sqrt{\frac{x^2}{4}+4x^2} \]
    \[ AM=\frac{x\sqrt{17}}{2} \]
  7. Use trigonometry

    In right triangle \(AMJ\),

    \[ \tan y^\circ=\frac{JM}{AM} \]
    \[ T=\frac{\frac{x(2+\sqrt3)}{2}}{\frac{x\sqrt{17}}{2}} \]
    \[ T=\frac{2+\sqrt3}{\sqrt{17}} \]
  8. Rationalise the denominator

    Rationalise the denominator:

    \[ T=\frac{(2+\sqrt3)\sqrt{17}}{17} \]
    \[ T=\frac{2\sqrt{17}+\sqrt{51}}{17} \]
    \[ T=\frac{\sqrt{68}+\sqrt{51}}{17} \]
Final answer

\(\displaystyle T=\frac{\sqrt{68}+\sqrt{51}}{17}\)

  1. Use trigonometry

    Use coordinates with

    \[ A=(0,0,0) \]
  2. Use trigonometry

    Since

    \[ AD=12,\qquad DE=20,\qquad AB=8 \]
  3. take
    \[ B=(0,0,8) \]
  4. The centre of the rectangular

    \[ M=(6,10,0) \]
  5. Find the midpoint

    \[ C=(12,0,8),\qquad F=(12,20,8) \]
    \[ P=(12,10,8) \]
  6. Use trigonometry

    Now
